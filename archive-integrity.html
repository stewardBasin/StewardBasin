Steward Basin Public Archive Verification Ledger
Steward Basin preserves public environmental records using cryptographic integrity verification standards designed to ensure records remain authentic, unchanged, and independently verifiable over time.

All archived records are cryptographically verified and preserved locally.

Archive Standard

  • Level 1 → manually collected
  • Level 2 → archived locally
  • Level 3 → public source identified
  • Level 4 → source + local archive preserved
  • Level 5 → SHA256 checksum verified
