Title: Duchesne County - Treated Water Release

Date: 05/13/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration & Production Company

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a leak in a treated water line from RO system entering location 5-32-8-17. Approximately 95 barrels was released to a dry drainage. The line was isolated and shutdown to stop the release. Release will be assessed in the morning to determine remediation which will most likely be flushing or tilling. A sample will be collected to verify remediation effectiveness including a background sample.
