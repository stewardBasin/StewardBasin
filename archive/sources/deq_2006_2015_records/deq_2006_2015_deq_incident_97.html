Incident Summary

Per NRC report: Release from a condensate above ground storage tank at El Paso E&P Compressor Station was caused by a one-inch valve. Approximately 8 barrels of condensate leaked into an unknown tributary of the Duchesne River. The material has been contained in a ditch using booms and is currently being cleaned up with VAC trucks. No material was released into the river. It is unclear if this event is reportable. Notification is being made as a precautionary measure. Color of material is brown with sheen 50ft x 10ft. UDOGM is on-scene.
