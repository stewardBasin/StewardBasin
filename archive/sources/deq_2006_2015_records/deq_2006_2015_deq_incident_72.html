Incident Summary

Caller witnessed white tanker trucks dumping an unknown liquid substance into what he referred to as "Kids" canal at 1500 West near 500 South (behind a movie theater complex). He saw trucks doing this on at least two occasions: Thursday (Oct 16) at 6:30 pm and again the next day Friday (Oct. 17) at 12:30 pm. The white trucks were the rounded tanker-type that backed up to the canal and released a liquid the caller described as strong, pungent, and possibly "sulfurous or solvent-like." Trucks had no visible names or numbers on them that the caller could see. The caller believes the canal eventually flows into Steinaker Reservoir. Caller said he contacted dispatch with the County Sheriff's office (435-789-2511 / or -4222) and left his call back number but never got a return call from them. He attempted to call the Ashley Valley Water Conservancy District (435-789-3212, or 435-789-1651) but said their office was closed. Caller says so far there has been no response to his query. Caller speculated that the liquid wastes being dumped may be oil-field related.
