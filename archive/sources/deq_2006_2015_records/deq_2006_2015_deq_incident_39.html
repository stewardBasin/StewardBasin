Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of approximately 25 gallons of condensate from a crack in a gathering line at location Lat 40d 23.445m Long -109d 54.862m. Material spilled to an isolated closed dead-end ditch with dimensions of 4ft x 20ft x 6ft. Ditch had stagnant water prior to spill. VAC truck was dispatched to collect all free liquid. Backhoe will excavate/scrape residual tomorrow. There are no surface waters nearby and no waterways were impacted.
