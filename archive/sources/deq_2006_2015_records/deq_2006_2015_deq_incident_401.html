Incident Summary

From Dennis Ingram of DOGM and Scot Ghan with BBC (Scott's phone number is 970-744-8128). He was scrambling to get all the details also. Howell Trucking lost a truck with a pup trailer carrying production water off a dirt road in the Rabbit Gulch area north of Starvation Reservoir. Reported location (approximate) is attached via a Google Earth placemark. This happened yesterday afternoon. Not sure how much was released, but it did run for about 1/4 mile apparently, so maybe 100 bbls or so until we know better. It was reported that surface water was not impacted. The truck was carrying Bill Barrett Corp's production water, so even though the fault was Howell Trucking (pulling off the road to let somebody pass, and pulled too far off the road) BBC is addressing the assessment and cleanup with their environmental consultant who should be onsite today. This spill is in a source protection area for Starvation Reservoir, which is a drinking water source. I am going to visit the site this afternoon to make sure it did not reach the reservoir. I was told it did not but I think we need to confirm that, and see how close it did come. From Google Earth it looks like it was something like 1.3 miles to the reservoir from the spill site. Apparently a dry wash, I would like to confirm that also. I will send out a report late today when I know more. -Scott
