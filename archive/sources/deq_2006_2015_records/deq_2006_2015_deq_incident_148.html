Incident Summary

Caller is reporting the release of approximately 15 gallons of hyrdraulic fluid at Western United Electric facility. Release was from a diesel tractor truck caused by mechanical failure. Impact is limited to gravel and dirt. No impact reported to surface water/drains at this time. Lincoln Environmental has been dispatched to perform cleanup.
