Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 8815

Title: Duchesne Diesel Release

Date: 11/14/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Bill Barrett Resources

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 40 gallons of diesel from a fuel tank at an oil/gas rig. Fuel tank supplied fuel to a generator and the release occurred when a connection line leaked. Diesel fuel released to standing storm-water at the site and was contained with the use of absorbant pads. No water bodies were impacted. Release location described as approximately 3 miles west of Duchesne, Utah. Release occurred on tribal lands.
