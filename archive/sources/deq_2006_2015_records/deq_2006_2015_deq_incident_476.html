Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11520

Title: Injection Water Release

Date: 08/28/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration & Production Co

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Injection line broke releasing about 10 barrels of water. An estimated 5 barrels flowed across the road into a waterway fed by an emergent spring. This flows to a stock pond. All injection water was processing through their boundary injection facility consisting of water drawn only from the Green River. System was shut off at about midnight. There are crop fields to the north of the break. Waterway eventually flows through the Pariette Draw then into the Pariette Wetlands about 5-6 miles downstream.
