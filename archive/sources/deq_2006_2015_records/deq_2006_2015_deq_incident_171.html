Title: Duchesne County - Injection Water Release

Date: 02/20/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.052211666581, -110.037473905155

Incident Summary

Caller reported an injection water release of ~30 barrels into a dry drainage near well location 4-8-9-17 on 2/20/2014 at ~400. The release was discovered on 2/21/2014 at ~900. 10 barrels were recovered from the road while 20 barrels flowed to the dry drainage. No waterways were impacted. Caller said cleanup should start 2/21/2014 with fresh water followed by lab analysis of soil samples.
