Incident Summary

Upon arriving at the Sears store in Vernal to deliver goods, a leak was discovered in the vehicles fuel line. The release was determined to have been about 15 gallons of diesel fuel. Rains occuring in the area washed the fuel down a nearby storm drain. The caller stated she was in the process of hiring a contractor to address the situation.
