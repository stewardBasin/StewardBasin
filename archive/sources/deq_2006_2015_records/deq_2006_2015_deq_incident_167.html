Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11934

Title: Duchesne Co - Crude & Produced Water

Date: 08/25/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: TALMAGE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Approximately 225 barrels of crude oil and 413 barrels of produced water was released from the Lawrence 1-30B4 tank battery on 8/25/2014 at approximately 1:30 PM. The release was the result of equipment failure cuased by a storm the preceding night. The release migrated off-site. Spill response actions were implemented. Berms were construction in the spill pathway as soon as practicable and cleanup has commenced. Confirmatory post-cleanup samples will be collected by a third party (Kleinfelder) and a copy of the results will be submitted to Tri-County Health. No surface waters were impacted. Materials did migrate approximately 400-500 yards out into a sagebrush plain. Spill was originally reported to DOGM about 15:00 on Monday. Cleanup is underway and additional berms have been made to improve containment since additional rain is anticipated. Property immediately downstream of the release is owned by EP Energy (40 acre parcel). Some of the material migrated off-site. PRP is working to determine and contact the affected property owner to gain access for cleanup. DE met with PRP rep on-site late Tuesday.
