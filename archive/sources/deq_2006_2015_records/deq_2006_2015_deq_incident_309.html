Title: Myton - Newfield Collection Line Release

Date: 04/10/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.064654481715, -109.961408866233

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release from a gathering line in the 8 Mile Flat North Field. The gathering line runs along the access road to well "Federal 4-1-9-17" located at UTM E (588571) and UTM N (4435450). Release was from a crack in the line that sprayed oil on the side of a hill that then ran down along the road and into a dry drainage. No water was impacted. The line was secured and the cleanup started. A clearance survey was immediately initiated to identify any species of concern that might be impacted. They were able to locate 4 Cacti that are listed as endangered, but none were in the spill area. They are maintaining surveillance of these plants insure they remain safe. The Majority of the cleanup is expected to be complete before wet weather moves into the area over the weekend. Newfield notified DOGM and the BLM who have had some one on site on the 10th.
