Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11547

Title: Ethylene Glycol Release

Date: 09/14/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that 1030 gallons of ethylene glycol were released at a well site approximately 8 to 10 miles southwest of Myton. The RQ for ethylene glycol is 5000 pounds. The caller reported that it weighs 9.29 pounds per gallon making the release over 9568 pounds. The glycol ran off the well site and down a road and into a dry drainage, there was no water in the drainage at the time. It then flowed approximately 150 feet down the drainage. Approximately 200 gallons entered the drainage. The drainage was dammed and the flow stopped. They used a pump truck to suck up all the liquid in the drainage then began to remove the contaminated soil. The soil will be stockpiled at the well site then transferred to Environmental Energy Inovations where it will be remediated. The well site was number 8-33-8-16 and is located south east of the north east quarter quarter of section 33, 8 south 16 east.
