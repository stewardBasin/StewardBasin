Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_208

Title: Production Water Release in Duchesne County

Date: 08/01/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.097168549705, -110.036348009779

Incident Summary

Caller reported a spill of approximately 150 barrels of brackish production water called "schmoo" (pipe corrosion) from well #13-20-8-17. The spill flowed 1700 feet into a dry drainage ditch and did not quite reach a dry stock pond. No waterways were impacted. Caller said that the water soaked into the soil and left a black brackish material on the surface of the soil. This will be excavated and properly disposed of.
