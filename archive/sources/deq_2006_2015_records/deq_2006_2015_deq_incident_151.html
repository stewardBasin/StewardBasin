Incident Summary

Caller reports about 15 barrels of injection water was released near well 4-32-8-17 under a road to a drainage. Due to the heavy rains, the drainage was flowing. Released water was carried downstream with existing stream flow. Drainage flows downstream to Pariette Wetlands. Injection water is drawn from the Green River.
