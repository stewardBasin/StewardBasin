Title: Duchesne Co - Treated Injection Water

Date: 09/15/2015

County: Duchesne

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting the release of approximately 98 barrels of treated injection water. Water flowed onto a road were it pooled. Crew was able to recover approximately 50 barrels. Remaining volume flowed off the road and onto flat land were it absorbed. Cause of the release is due to internal pipe corrosion. No impact to surface waterways.
