Title: Roosevelt - Condensate release

Date: 10/26/2013

County: Uintah

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a truck rollover resulted in the release of 5-7 bbls. condensate. Rollover caused a valve on the truck to open, resulting in the release. Responsible party is awaiting Blue Stakes clearance before excavating impacted soils. Responsible party agreed to provide a clean-up report to the Tri-County Health Department within 30 days.
