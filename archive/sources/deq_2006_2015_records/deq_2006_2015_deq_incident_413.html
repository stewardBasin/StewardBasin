Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of paraffin wax crude from a ruptured disk that blew on their separator. 15 gallons of crude sprayed around the separator coating the snow. No waterways or surface water was impacted. A vacuum truck is en-route to clean the surface and impacted soils will be excavated and disposed. The disk is being repaired and investigating a possible frozen fuel line that caused the rupture.
