Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_410

Title: 80 Barrel Injection Water Release

Date: 08/27/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Drilling Services

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.089992386589, -109.979017971216

Incident Summary

Split in an injection line resulted in the release of 80 barrels of injection water that ran (with rain water) 1586 feet down a dry ditch. Both DOGM and BLM have been notified by the spiller. A cleanup team will enter the area after the rain has stopped due to potential impacts to an endangered cactus.
