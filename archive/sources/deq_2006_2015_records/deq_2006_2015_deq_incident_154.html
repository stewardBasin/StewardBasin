Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11369

Title: Diesel Fuel Spill in Duchesne County

Date: 06/13/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Trimac

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported an estimated 10-15 gallons of diesel fuel that was released to the road and soil. This occurred when part of the road that a tractor trailer was driving on gave way causing the trailer to lean and spill its fuel. No drains or waterways were impacted. Absorbents have been placed on the spill. Lincoln Environmental was contacted and will be on site for clean-up in about 3 hours from the time of the call.
