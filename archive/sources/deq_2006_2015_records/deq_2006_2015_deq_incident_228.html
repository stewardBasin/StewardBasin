Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_228

Title: Tri-County - Newfield Pipeline release

Date: 11/04/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.050868851552, -110.013858140465

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a break in a 6-inch pipeline resulted in the release of 158 barrels (bbl) of treated injection water. Released material ran down the shoulder of a roadway and crossed the road via a culvert. Released material then entered a stock-watering pond. Well location is 21-9H-9-17. Release occurred on BLM property. Newfield notified the BLM, DOGM and Tri-County DE Scott Hacking of the release. Newfield will coordinate with these agencies for a site visit later today.
