Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11687

Title: Duchesne Co - Sodium Hypochlorite 12.5% 1200 Gals

Date: 01/19/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration & Production Co

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Tote with 12.5% sodium hypochlorite was ruptured when struck by the tine of a forklift. About 1,200 lbs (Vol ~ 2.5 bbls) of solution was released. All was contained on-site at location 4-14-3-2. Will remediate by VAC truck (100 bbl volume), top off with water. Tentatively plan to deliver to a lined disposal pond upon approval of appropriate authorities. Land is private with regulatory oversight by DOGM.
