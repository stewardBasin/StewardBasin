Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_201

Title: Vernal - Hydrochloric Acid Solution

Date: 07/17/2013

County: Uintah

Company: Baker Hughes Trucking Service

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.386778146218, -109.438909573562

Incident Summary

Driver of a tractor trailer was backing into a parking stall at the Split Mountain Truck Stop in Vernal when a tote containing a 15% Hydrochloric solution fell off the truck to the asphalt. Quantity released was 240 gallons. Environmental Restoration was contracted for clean-up services and is en-route to the scene. No injuries were reported.
