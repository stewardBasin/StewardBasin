Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Driver was attempting to unload diesel into bulk plant. Driver mistakenly set pump to pull diesel out of bulk plant instead of pumping into bulk plant. Diesel filled drivers 'pup' trailer and began to flow out of the 'pup' vent stack. Approximately 450 gallons were released onto the ground from the drivers 'pup'. Western Petroleum (WP) came out and recovered approximately 300 gallons of diesel from the spill area. On Sunday (01/17/09) a crew member on site observed diesel pooling in the area and absorbent material was used to gather pooling diesel. On Monday (01/20/09) crew used a backhoe to scrap and stockpile the affected soil for shipment to the local landfill. Darin Brown of the Tri-County Health Department was on site to observe the soil removal. Soil remains on site pending analytical results required for disposal at landfill.
