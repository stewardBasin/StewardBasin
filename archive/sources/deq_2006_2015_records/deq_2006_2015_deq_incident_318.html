Incident Summary

Approximiately 345 gallons of diesel fuel was spilled on locaton while a truck driver accidentally reversed the pump and pumped fuel from the vapor valve on the pup trailer. Western Petroleum did respond and hired a contractor to clean it up. Tri-CountyI looked at it after the cleanup and it didn't appear to get in any surface water of storm drain. The cleanup report is pending. The contaminated soil was taken to the landfill. Western Petroleum did not report the spill until days later.
