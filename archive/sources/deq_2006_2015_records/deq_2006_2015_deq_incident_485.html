Title: Duchesne County - Production Water Spill

Date: 08/05/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.213605842655, -110.161394437864

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 35-barrel production water spill from a production water line. The spill went to a drainage ditch that is normally dry but because of the recent storms, the ditch contained rainwater. Because of the rain, some of the spill (less than 5 barrels) was flushed onto tribal lands. Caller said that the impacted area is being flushed and vacuumed to retrieve as much production water as possible but the area is muddy so clean-up has been difficult.
