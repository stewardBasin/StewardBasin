Incident Summary

The operator reached to close a one inch valve on a filter housing and touched the hot piping. The valve did not get closed completely. Four barrels of triethylene glycol was released and approximately 2 barrels made it off-site. The release filled the containment and ran off the facility to the North. The release ran down a wash approximately 100 feet. The operator was able to dam up the wash and contained the release. Soaker pads were laid down to absorbed as much liquid as possible. Three loads of fresh water was then used to wash the rocks and drainage off. The mix was then sucked up and hauled to a disposal facility. There was some ice that formed while cleaning the spill so another truckload of fresh water will be used to wash any remaining contaminants when things thaw out. Notification was sent to the district engineer and DEQ from DOGM directly by e-mail.
