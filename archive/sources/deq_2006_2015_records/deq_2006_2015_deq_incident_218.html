Incident Summary

Caller reported that the Colorado Highway Patrol received an anonymous report (NRC Incident Report #890340) for a spill of unknown oil along Highway 40. A Colorado HazMat team was dispatched to the scene and found the spill on the Utah side of the border. The spill appeared to have originated in a rest area/turnout and continued out into the road surface. The spill was estimated to be several weeks to a month old and had soaked into the road surface. Caller notified the NRC to correct the incident location. Caller stated that the State of Colorado has seen an increase in oil-related spills along this particular corridor. Because the initial NRC Incident Report #890340 described the spill as being inside the State of Colorado, the UDEQ has no record of this report.
