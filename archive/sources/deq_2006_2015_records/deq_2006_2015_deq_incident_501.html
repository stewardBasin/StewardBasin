Incident Summary

Ashley upper canal workers went to work on a canal outlet and discovered someone had dumped sludge around the concrete lateral exiting the canal. A recent dump occurred with in the last 24 hours, but previous loads have been dumped there. Gray and black in color and odorous. fifty feet from a resident. Dump occurred on Millecan's property. They are out of town on vacation.
