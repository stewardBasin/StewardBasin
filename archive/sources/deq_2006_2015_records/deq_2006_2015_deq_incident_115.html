Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11896

Title: Vernal Used Oil Spill

Date: 07/22/2014

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Precision Asphalt

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A residence called and complained about oil coming into her yard at about 100 west and 250 North. When I visited the site, it appeared to be used oil in her yard. The oil was on the surface of the water coming from the curb and gutter storm water. I traced it back to 462 West 100 North, where someone had dumped at least 5 gallons of oil in the gutter. The resident was not home, and the neighbor said they had left on vacation to Montana. There wast a vehicle in the driveway with a company name on it of Precision Asphalt. It could be some other petroleum used in that business. I will followup when they are home to see if I can get them to clean it up.
