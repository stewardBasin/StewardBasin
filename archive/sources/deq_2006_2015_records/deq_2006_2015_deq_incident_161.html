Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11718

Title: Duchesne Co. - Injection water spill

Date: 02/16/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported an injection water spill of approximately 120 barrels into a dry drainage ditch. No waterways were impacted. Caller said the area impacted by the spill will be flushed with clean water and then sampled for lab analysis.
