Title: Duchesne County Produced Water Spill

Date: 02/06/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.012325432158, -110.167636037561

Incident Summary

Caller reported that approximately 8 barrels of produced water leaked from an injection line. The water ran down a road, into a dry drainage and back onto the same road where it stopped. Due to the small amount of material they plan to take soil samples from the drainage to determine if there was any impact. If there was impact they will flush with clean water. The leak occurred at well number 13-19-9-16. There was no threat to surface or ground water.
