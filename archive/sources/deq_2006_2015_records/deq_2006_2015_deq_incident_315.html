Incident Summary

Reporting party is making initial notification of a mercury spill on concrete along Main Street in Vernal. Information is extremely limited at this time. Local responders have been notified and are enroute at this time. Caller will notify the NRC and gather more information and call back. DEQ has recommended (1) notification to the National Response Center, (2) gather add'l info address of incident and indoor or outdoor, (3) isolate and secure the area, (4) determine the amount spilled. Local responders may be able to initiate a response if resources - for example - if powdered sulfur is available locally. UPDATE 12:25 - Volume released is estmated at three tablespoons on the sidewalk. Deputy Director Env. Health is on-site. 13:00 LHD rep on-site reports that the mercury was found on a set of wooden stairs on a private building adjacent to a public building. Most of the mercury is trapped in the wood of the stairs and decking at the top with some observed on the concrete beneath the structure. UPDATE January 30, 2007 9:10 a.m.>>>>>>>>>>>>>>> Darrin Brown reports that the owner of the property Mr."Dennis Judd" will make a decision today as to who will cleanup the mercury spill. EPA is unwilling to cleanup the spill using EPA funds because the spill is on business property. Mr. Judd has received several bids for the cleanup, but feels that all bids are too expensive. The spill area has been fenced and cordoned off to eliminate public exposure.
