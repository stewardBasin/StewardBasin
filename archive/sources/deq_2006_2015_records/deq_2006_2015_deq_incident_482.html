Incident Summary

Caller reported that Newfield Production Company has been drilling an oil well within a few hundred feet near his property. Based on the provided address information it is assumed that the oil well is Jorgensen 2-4-9-3-2WH (API 4301352107). During the drilling process the caller noticed changes in the flow to the private well (depth~225') on his property. Caller has also siphoned water from the well to irrigate a pond (~12'x12'x2') stocked with gold fish. On the incident discovery date caller observed that the pond had a sheen and the the fish were dead. Also, Newfield appears to have completed the well based on observations by the caller.
