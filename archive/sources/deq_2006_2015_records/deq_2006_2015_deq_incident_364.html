Title: Produced Water Spill Near Myton

Date: 02/23/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.073505283867, -110.078994236115

Incident Summary

Piping sprang a leak releasing about 30 barrels of treated produced water. The water flowed about 3,000 feet to a dry drainage. The impacted soil will be flushed with clean water and a sample analyzed to verify remediation effectiveness.
