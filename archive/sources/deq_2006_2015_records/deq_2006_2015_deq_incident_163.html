Title: Uintah Co - Used Oil Spill

Date: 12/21/2013

County: Uintah

Company: Industrial Horse Power

Nearest City: NAPLES

Coordinates: 40.441220031087, -109.501049205631

Incident Summary

Used oil spill at an engine repair shop. A fork lift driver punctured a tote releasing 185 gallons of used oil to a dirt surface at their location. About 85 gallons was recovered. Absorbent was placed over the area and the contaminated absorbent and soil is being placed in a container. About a 20'X20' area was impacted and the oil stayed onsite. TriCounty Health is overseeing cleanup.
