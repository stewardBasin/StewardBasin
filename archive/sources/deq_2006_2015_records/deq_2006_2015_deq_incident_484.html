Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11975

Title: Vernal - Release of "OB Breaker"

Date: 09/22/2014

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Nabors Well Service

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a well company discharged a bright aqua blue hazardous substance, called OB Breaker, to the ground from a sump. The well company then vacuumed up the freestanding liquid but did not clean-up the impacted soil. Caller did not know the amount released but said that it covered an area of about 100' x 200' in size. Caller did not know if this substance has impacted any waterways but said that it has been raining heavily lately in the Vernal region.
