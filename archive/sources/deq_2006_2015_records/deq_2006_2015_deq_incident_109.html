Title: Duchesne Co - Treated Injection Water

Date: 05/07/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Incident Summary

Treated injection water leaked from a line. The water flowed off location to a nearby dry drainange. Estimated volume is 20 barrels. Area has received a lot of rain (not enough to maintain flow in the impacted drainage). Water infiltrated. Ground is soft and muddy. Leak has been isolated. Near well site No. 1A-36-8-16.
