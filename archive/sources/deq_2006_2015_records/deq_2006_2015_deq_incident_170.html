Title: Uintah Co- Injection Water Release

Date: 10/12/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

50 bbls of injection water at well site 12-18-9-16. NW of SW Section 18 T9S R16 E. About half of release flowed to three dry drainages and the other half flowed to another nearby well site (5-18-9-16). Latter half is standing water. Response will recover for processing. Plan to collect soil and water samples.
