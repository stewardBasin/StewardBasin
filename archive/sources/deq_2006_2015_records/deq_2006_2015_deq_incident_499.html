Incident Summary

Caller reported approximately 100 gallons of diesel was released when an above ground storage tank (AST) was overfilled. Release was due to operator error. Company initially believed the AST was installed inside concrete containment system and sent a vac-truck to recover the diesel. Vac-truck driver observed that the AST did not have concrete containment and that the release had seeped into the surrounding soils. Remedial activities will proceed to address contaminated soils.
