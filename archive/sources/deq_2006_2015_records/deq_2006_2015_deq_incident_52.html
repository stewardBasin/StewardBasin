Incident Summary

Caller reported corrosion within a pipeline resulted in the release of 226 bbls of treated injection water. An estimated 130 bbls remained on-site, but 96 bbls ran off-site and into a nearby dry drainage. Crews have started clean-up of the release. Caller will notify Scott Hacking (DEQ), Richard Powell (DOGM), and Rany Bywater (BLM). Release location is well 13-27-8-17. Lat/Long: 40deg 5' 2.74" / -109deg 59" 58.88"
