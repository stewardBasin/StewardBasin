Title: Roosevelt Manure Truck wahsout to creek

Incident Summary

Attached is the email report submitted by Scott Hacking Darrin and I witnessed this incident firsthand late yesterday, at approximately 4:30pm. Photos with captions are at: https://picasaweb.google.com/105377044848809547245/WintertonTruckingSpill?authuser=0&authkey=Gv1sRgCIfr7LCw8tmdCw&feat=directlink Attached is a Google Earth placemark showing the location. This occurred on a small tributary to Dry Gulch Creek, just west of Roosevelt. Darrin and I were driving by on US-40 approximately 4:30pm yesterday (June 5th) when we saw a cattle hauling truck to the north of the highway that had backed up to the small creek and was obviously being washed out. We turned off the highway to investigate and found that a Winterton Trucking cattle hauling truck was in the process of being washed out into the small creek (see photos). The water coming out of the truck was heavily laden with manure and it was inundating the small creek with manure and probably ammonia also (from the urine). We observed dead and dying minnows in the small creek directly downstream of this, where the small creek was choked with manure. We talked to a representative of the trucking company that was present and told them this activity was not allowed, and they agreed to stop the activity. Apparently there had been several cattle trucks washed out at this location over the course of several days, in addition to the truck we observed being washed out. No water samples were taken, as it did not seem necessary given the firsthand observations. Any sample collected would have contained a heavy fraction of solids, due to the heavy manure load in the creek that this event caused. The creek immediately downstream was more of a manure slurry than water. I think we could sample this location for weeks to come and see the negative effects. Please advise if you want us to do that. Wintertons do own the land where this incident occurred. It is open to public access, and there are no gates; there is direct access off of US-40. As you know, Darrin sent an email earlier today recommending an NOV for this incident, and I agree completely. Downstream this incident probably killed on the order of a hundred minnows, and it will negatively impact the creek for some time to come. The amount of manure being washed out totally overwhelmed the small creek, and it will probably be many months before the Creek and its associated ecosystem can recover. This was also a very blatant incident, occurring adjacent to the Roosevelt City Limits and only about 150-200 yards off of US-40.
