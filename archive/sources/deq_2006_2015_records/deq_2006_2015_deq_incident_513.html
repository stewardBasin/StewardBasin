Title: Uintah County Production Water Spill

Date: 02/07/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.078753720023, -109.929680802487

Incident Summary

Caller reported that approximately 60 barrels of treated injection water leaked from a broken pipe and ran into a dry drainage. Three to five barrels of this was production water, the rest was clean water. They were able to recover approximately 40 barrels. They are flushing the drainage with clean water and will take soil samples afterwards. The leak occurred at well number 1-31-8-18. There was no threat to surface or ground water.
