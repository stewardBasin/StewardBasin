Incident Summary

Caller reported a produced water leak from a 2 7/8-inch buried pipeline utilized for salt water disposal due to unknown causes. Leak starts at latitude 40 14 08.25 North longitude 110 27 56.84 West and ends at latitude 40 14 1.92 North longitude 110 27 31.5 West. The leak is located near a dry wash approximately 0.8-mile upgradient of Starvation Reservoir.
