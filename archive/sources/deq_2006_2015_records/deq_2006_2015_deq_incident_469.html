Title: Myton - Injection water spill

Date: 07/28/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.036817415706, -110.059784750114

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 150 barrels of treated injection water (one-third treated injection water and two-thirds fresh water) that ran off the right-of-way into a dry drainage ditch. Clean-up is underway. They flushed the area today and will obtain soil samples for lab analysis. No waterways were impacted. Caller also contacted Richard Powell with DOGM and Randy Bywater with the BLM.
