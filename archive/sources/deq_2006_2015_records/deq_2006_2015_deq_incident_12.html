Incident Summary

Caller reported two injection water spills into dry drainage ditches that occurred yesterday. The first spill was 50 barrels at 6 a.m. and the second spill was 5 barrels at 7 a.m. These occurred south of Myton about 3/4-miles apart from one another. Caller said they will flush the impacted area with clean water and then sample the area for lab analysis. GPS coordinates that caller provided did not provide a valid location on map; therefore, location on map is approximate.
