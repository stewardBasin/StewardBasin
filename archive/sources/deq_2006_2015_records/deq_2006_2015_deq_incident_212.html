Incident Summary

The caller stated that a driver was filling some tanks inside a building at the bulk terminal facility and overfilled the tanks. Approximately 50 gallons of new oil was released from tank vent lines outside the building onto gravel and soil. Environmental Restoration has been contacted and will be doing the clean up. The caller stated that to her knowledge no waterways or drains were impacted.
