Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of diesel fuel occurred during a fuel delivery to the Laverne and Shirley’s retail fuel station. The quantity of diesel released was not known however the responding Vernal City Fire Dept. was able to contain contaminated soils inside a bucket for disposal at the landfill. Vernal City can be contacted for further details on this incident (435) 789-2255. Laverne and Shirley’s contact is (435) 789-3929.
