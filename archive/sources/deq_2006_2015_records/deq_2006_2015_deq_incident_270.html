Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11959

Title: Roosevelt Soil Contamination

Date: 09/09/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Kinder Morgan

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Mike Cronin with Wasatch Environmental called to report a spill that a property owner has contacted Wasatch about for 3rd party sampling. The property owner is Jared Jensen and Mike asked that I call him for additional details. I called Jared Jensen for the following report - Jared Jensen owns the property where a water line is being installed and discovered a crude oil or condensate release while digging. The contractor is Goram Construction and Jared claims they were told to remove impacted soils and finish the water line. Several hundred yards of impacted soils have been hauled off to the contractors yard and several hundred yards of impacted soils are stockpiled onsite. Jared said the release is impacting the Roosevelt Lateral, a ditch that flows into Pick-Up Wash and then to the Green River. I asked him to report to the NRC. Jared said the line was owned by El Paso Energy and recently purchased by Kinder Morgan. He also said there have been several releases or line breaches in the past few years that have gone unreported. He has lost several cattle and they are having blind calves... Email from Mike Cronin - Morgan, Here are the pictures that were sent to me today by the property owner. The property owner is Jared Jensen (435) 621-6517. He owns a ranch of about 600 acres on which the release occurred. He claims impacted soil is being disposed on the subcontractors property, that the release was not reported, and that there is no regulatory oversight . Potential RPs would be El Paso Energy or Kinder-Morgan. The release is condensate from a natural gas pipeline near Roosevelt, Utah. Let me know if you need anything else from me. Thanks, Mike FROM THE NRC REPORT - CALLER STATED THAT A CONDENSATE LINE THAT WAS NEVER CLEANED OUT LEAKED INTO THE GROUND ON THEIR PROPERTY. CALLER ALSO STATED THE MATERIAL WAS DISCHARGING INTO THE ROOSEVELT LATERAL WATERWAY WHICH LEADS TO THE COLORADO RIVER. CALLER STATED THE MATERIAL CAME OUT OF A BOOM BOOM WELL ON THE PROPERTY. STRAIGHT SHOT ROUST ABOUT COMPANY IS CURRENTLY EXCAVATING FOR KINDERMORGAN AT THIS TIME.
