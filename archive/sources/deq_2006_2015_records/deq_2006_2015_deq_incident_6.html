Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11763

Title: Duchesne Co - Crude Oil - 60 bbls

Date: 03/18/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Wasatch Tank Lines Doug Cook

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

2 barrels of crude leaking from a pup rollover that fell off of a semi and cracked. A secondary containment has been put in place and a pump truck attempted to remove crude but not successful. Owner is enroute to determine best way to clean up spill and remove cracked pup. DEQ District Engineer inspected site. Estimated volume of release is 60 barrels (75-80 barrels being hauled in the pup). Response crew constructed secondary berm containment to capture the release.
