Incident Summary

Caller reported that a storage tank leaked 315 barrels of oil into the berm surrounding the tank. The oil is contained within the berm and is not in or threatening any surface or ground water at this time. Clean-up crews will be dispatched in the morning to clean up the spill. The spill is at well site: Hendel 1-9-9-19 with coordinates 40.03965 N 109.79324 W.
