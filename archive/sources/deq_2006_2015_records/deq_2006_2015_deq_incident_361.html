Report #: deq_incident_361

Title: Production Water Spill

Date: 08/22/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Bill Barret Corporation

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.160663642757, -110.546803138481

Incident Summary

While transporting produced water to a SWD facility, a Howell Trucking water truck lost control of the vehicle and rolled over. As a result, ~158 bbls. of produced water were released onto the ground running into the roadside ditch. The spill travelled ~350' south in the roadside ditch before running down the eastern slope approximately 25' before stopping. The release occurred approximately 1.7 miles south of the intersection of Strawberry River Rd. and US 40. BBC notified Duchesne County of the release after it occurred. The accident/release was verbally reported to Dennis Ingram on 8/23/13 at ~8:30 A.M. Diversion structures were constructed to hold the pooled produced water until it was recovered with another water truck. All standing water was removed by a backup water truck. No visible hydrocarbon impact was observed on the surface within the spill path. Soil samples were collected along the entire spill path to evaluate for potential impacts. The laboratory results of these samples are pending and BBC will share these results with UDOGM upon receipt.
