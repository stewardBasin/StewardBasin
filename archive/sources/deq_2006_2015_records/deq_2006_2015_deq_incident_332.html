Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12420

Title: Newfield - Treated Injection Water

Date: 11/06/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 40 barrels (bbl) of treated injection water. Some of the material released off the property and flowed into a dry wash. A combination of vac trucks, flushing with fresh water, and manual excavation will be used to remediate the release. Caller additionally notified Scott Hacking (DEQ), Richard Powell (DOGM) and Randy Bywater (BLM). Release location is 6-28-8-17 (well is approx. 15 miles south of Myton).
