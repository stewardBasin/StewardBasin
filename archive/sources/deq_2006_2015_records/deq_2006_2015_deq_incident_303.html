Incident Summary

Approximately 500 gallons of used oil from the auto shop in the school coming out of a drian north of the auto shop. Used oil flowed down the storm drain gutter for approx 100 yrds did not enter the storm drain. Flow has been dammed and did not effect any waterway. The school district and Bugsy's Water Service are cleaning up the spill.
