Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water Release

Date: 08/30/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.014528033612, -110.201531168352

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release due to internal corrosion of piping resulting in about 93 bbls of treated injection water. The release was initiated from above the well site designated as 11-23-9-15 with coordinates of T9S R15E S23. The release flowed down to the road and flowed over down to where it stopped at the berm located at the well site. No waterways were impacted. The line was repaired and cleanup is in progress. The area will be flushed with fresh water after cleanup is completed likely later in the day.
