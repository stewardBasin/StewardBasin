Incident Summary

Caller reported a that a truck hauling radioactive material overturned along Highway 40. Initial reports indicate that the material breached the containment. An unknown liquid and powder material surrounds the crash site and EMS workers may have come into contact with these materials. Jeremy Raymond (Uintah County Fire Dept.) is on-scene and awaiting meters (Geiger counters) to survey the area. DERR contacted Bill Craig (Division Radiation Control - DRC). UPDATE 1 10/24/10 08:54: Bill Craig (DRC) reported that the truck rolled just west of Vernal on Highway 40. The radiation source did NOT breach containment and there are NO exposures resulting from the incident. The radiation source is owned by Quality Testing and they are on-scene working with local Emergency personnel. The source is Iridium 192 and is intact. I do not have the destination information for the source but the radiation source is a device used for strength-testing metals in production wells and welds in other metal components. Bill indicated these devices and their radiation sources are designed and secured during transport to withstand high impacts during accidents. The driver of the truck was reportedly drunk at the time of the crash and he was removed from the scene by ambulance to treat injuries from the accident but was not exposed to any radiation. Bill Craig (801) 565-1129 (home phone) has been in contact technical support and providing with the On-Scene Coordinator - Duchesne Fire Chief Jeremy Raymond. Gwen Galloway (DRC) home phone: (801) 964-2035 has taken over technical support duties and may visit the scene later today to provide regulatory oversight for handling of the source. All indications are that the situation is now under control. UPDATE 2 10/24/10 10:02: Liquid material involved in the spill appears to be a dilute bleach solution used in the development of images taken by the device. A contractor was hired to provide clean-up of the solution as well as any diesel released during the accident.
