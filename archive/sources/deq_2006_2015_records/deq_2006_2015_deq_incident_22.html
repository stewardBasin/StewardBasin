Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_22

Title: Myton Lift Station Release

Date: 09/16/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: K. W. Robinson Construction

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.200597683105, -110.047445136537

Incident Summary

Caller stated that they worked on a lift station repair job for Myton and it required them to pump out the whole every day. The old lift station was leaking into the excavation and they would pump it out into a farmer’s ditch that ran to the Duchesne River. Caller indicated there was inorganic material from the sewer in the release that was pumped to the ditch. It occurred approximately a month and a half ago (mid-September)
