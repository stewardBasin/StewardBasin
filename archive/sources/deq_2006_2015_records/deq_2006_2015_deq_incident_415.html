Report #: 12205

Title: Condensate Spill near Myton

Date: 05/20/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Monarch Natural Gas

Nearest City: MYTON

Incident Summary

Tank corrosion caused a tank to fail releasing about 1,600 gallons of condensate water to secondary containment. No water escaped containment. Address is T 9S R 18E Sec 36.
