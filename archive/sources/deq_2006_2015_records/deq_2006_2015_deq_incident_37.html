Title: Uintah Co - Ethylene Glycol 1000 Gallons

Date: 06/04/2013

County: Uintah

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of ethylene glycol with an approximate volume of 1,000 gallons. A flange connection on a process line ruptured causing the release. The material was contained to an on-site unlined manhole space at or near the point of release. 900 gallons of material (and some contaminated soils) was vacuumed and placed into liquid bins. UPDATE: Estimated total release revised to 1050. Crew anticipates total recovery will include an additional 30 gallons
