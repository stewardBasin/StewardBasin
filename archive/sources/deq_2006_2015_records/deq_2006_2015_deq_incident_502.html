Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_502

Title: Used Engine Gear Oil Spill

Date: 07/01/2013

County: Uintah

Company: Western Petroleum Logistics Services

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.433703004859, -109.499365236375

Incident Summary

330 Gallon tote of used drilling rig engine gear oil was being off-loaded with a fork-lift for transfer to a facility used oil tank. The tote slipped off the forks, tipped and spilled. Incident occurred within a concrete and asphalt and bermed containment area. An estimated five to ten gallons splashed to soils outside of the containment. Cleanup is underway. As of this time upwards of 200 gallons have been recovered. Absorbents and floor-dry have been deployed. Contaminated soils are being excavated and drummed for disposal. No impact to surface waters or sewers.
