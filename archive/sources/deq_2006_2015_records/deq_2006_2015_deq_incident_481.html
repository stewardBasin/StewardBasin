Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11620

Title: Duchesene County Treated Production Water Release

Date: 11/16/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported a release of 78 barrels of treated production water at well site number 2-36-8-16 located in the North West of the North East Quarter Quarter of Section 36. The map coordinates above are for the town of Myton because the caller did not have coordinates for the well site. The water ran into a dry drainage. Samples were taken with data expected to be available on November 18th. Clean up is underway. Once the clean up is complete the dry drainage will be flushed with clean water.
