Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 8854

Title: Rosevelt Top Stop Release

Date: 05/28/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Pioneer Wireline Services

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Release occurred at a Top-Stop facility. PRP as identifed, was refueling both diesel saddle tanks on a tractor. When driver discovered second line releasing he shut the line. An estimated 25-60 gallons of diesel was released. An estimated volume of thirty-one gallons flowed from the containment pad and entered a nearby creek. Fire Dept responded. Response crew has deployed booms and absorbents. Contractor (Envirocare) out of Salt Lake has been mobilized (E.T.A. is several hours).
