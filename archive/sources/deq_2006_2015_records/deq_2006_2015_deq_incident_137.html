Title: Duchesne Co - Sewage

Date: 09/27/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Unknown

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that his residence along with five other new residential structures are being inundated by high groundwater that is floating sewage from septic systems to flow near and into several of the homes. One house has basement and is flooded. Caller had contact with TriCounty Health Dept on prior similar circumstance. Caller stated that water/sewage is pooling in and around the homes. The issue threatens occupancy of all homes.
