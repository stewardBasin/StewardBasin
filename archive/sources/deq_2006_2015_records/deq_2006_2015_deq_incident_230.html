Incident Summary

Caller expressed concern for potential ground/surface water contamination. Caller stated he replaced a sump pump. Old pump had coat of hydrocarbon. When new pump was started, an odor of diesel, and a sheen on the pump discharge was observed. Caller stated, to maintain water levels, five residents in the area typically run sump pumps between Sept 1 and the freeze. Caller resides within 300-400 yards of wetlands and quarter-mile from Duchesne River.
