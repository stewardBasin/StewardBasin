Incident Summary

The caller is an attorney who reported this incident to DERR on behalf of his clients. His clients reported that they witnessed the release of oil production water from a water tanker and semi-truck onto a right-of-way access road on their property. They said that they were certain that the water was contaminated because it smelled like oil. The semi-truck is owned by Hot Oil Express, 6232 N 16500 W, Altamont, UT, 84001, phone: 435-454-3066. A police report was filed by the property owners. They also collected a soil sample for hydrocarbon analysis and took photos of the site. It is unknown if the contaminated water impacted any waterways.
