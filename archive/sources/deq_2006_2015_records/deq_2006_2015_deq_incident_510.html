Incident Summary

Caller reports a release of approximately 50 gallons of unleaded gasoline at facility fueling island. Operator started fueling and left the process unattended. (purchase was for 64.2 gallons). Fuel flowed into and was completed contained in a concrete drain (drain flows to grass, which was not impacted). On-site personnel immediately deployed absorbant onto the fuel. Naples Fire Dept. responded. As a result of the quick response by on-site personnel Naples Fire did not require any action on their part. Contaminated absorbant is being drummed and will be taken by Snelson.
