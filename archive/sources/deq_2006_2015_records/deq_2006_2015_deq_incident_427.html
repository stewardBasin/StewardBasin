Incident Summary

Caller was approached and contracted with the PRP for temporary use (about two months (beginning 11/16/12) of private land on which to stage exploratory geophysical operations 50 support and geophysical vehicles. Caller visited operations site and discovered and observed refueling operation associated with use of a helicopter. Caller suspected a lack of proper protocol for the operation because jet fuel was being sprayed during refueling of the helicopter. There was no secondary containment for the temporary landing site which conssisted only of 2x10 planks of wood. Caller said fuel was being transported in via truck and pumped across yards of gournd directly into the fuel tanks. Fuel would left to spray in the wind during the operation. Some materials left after demob included plywood that is saturated with petroleum. Vehciles were staged near a primary source canal. Caller said one truck was parked on the bank of the canal causing it to collapse. Caller stated a representative from DOGM was on-site during a meeting at the property and observed conditions.
