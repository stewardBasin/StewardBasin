Incident Summary

The phone number for Slate Resources is (435) 781-1870. Hector reported the spill on Monday the 9th, around 1pm. His phone number is (801) 259-6110. David Hackford of DOGM visited the site Tuesday morning at approximately 9am, and he reported that the 8-inch diameter pipeline was leaking a small amount of natural gas under relatively low pressure, and that some condensate liquids had leaked out of the pipeline and covered a small area of ground near the pipeline. Dave called Slate River's contractor (the "pumper", not sure who that was) and told them to fix the leak ASAP. SITLA staff (Sonja Wallace, Jim Davis and Scott Bartlett) and I also visited the site at approximately 11:30am on Tuesday, and found things as Dave had described. The contractor had not responded yet to fix the leak, but it was raining at the time and weather was a factor in getting equipment to the scene. The leak was small and not too serious, due to the low pressure in the line at the time. The condensate spill was very localized, as Dave had described. We did not collect any samples due to the very localized nature of the condensate spill, which had only run about 5 or 6 feet from the pipeline. The spill is small and should be very easy to clean up. Surface water was not affected, the nearest would be Willow Creek, which is over 3 miles away from this location. It was raining at the time, so there was some stormwater runoff. Darrin knows about this spill, he was going to join us for our site visit but had another commitment at the time. The easement for the pipeline is held by Slate River Resources, LLC, 418 E Main, Vernal, 84078. The easement was approved in February of 2006. It is described as an 8-inch natural gas gathering pipeline that transports gas from wells in the Agency Draw area to market. It crosses trust land in Section 2, T13S, R20E, and terminates in Section 35, T12S, R20E, where it connects to another pipeline.
