Incident Summary

Acid transport truck rolled into the bottom of a dry wash. About 1500 gallons of 28% hydrochloric acid was lost. Acid had been contained and will be neutralized today. UPDATE 1/17/06 16:00 from Becky Hackford (Tri-County Health Dept.): TW Company responded to the scene to clean-up hydrochloric acid spill. Ray Maxon (TW Company (801) 641-1269) is the on-scene contact. TW Company reported a total of 960 gallons was released and had traveled 450 feet from the point of spill. The spilled acid had absorbed 6 inches into the ground. Caustic soda was used to neutralize the spill.
