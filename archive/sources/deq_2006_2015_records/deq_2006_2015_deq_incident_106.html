Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_106

Title: Injection water release near Myton

Date: 11/24/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.07244895905, -109.880314340596

Incident Summary

A well injection line broke when fresh water was flowing through the line. Approximately 90 barrels was lost, flowed down a dry wash, melted snow and ice in the draw and flowed into Pariette Draw. Some of the water was discolored with corrosion products. This will be washed off rocks near the break.
