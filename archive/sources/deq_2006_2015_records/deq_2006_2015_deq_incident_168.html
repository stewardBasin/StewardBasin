Incident Summary

ATV rider notified the Utah Dept. of Natural Resources DWR of the use of approximately 3 tablespoon volume of mercury situated in a cast iron pan inside an experimental flotation device that was anchored in the Green River. The rider oberved the device in the water and inspected it. Rider recognized material in the device as mercury and removed it to prevent a its release into the river. The owner of the device was in the area and confronted the rider about disturbing his device. The rider advised the owner about introducing chemicals in to the waterway. Rider learned from owner that device later observed the owner later removing the device. At the time of the report the mercury was turned over to DWR conservation officer and it has been reliquished to TRICounty Envrionmental Health for proper materials management.
