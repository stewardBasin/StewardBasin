Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11899

Title: Duchesne Diesel Release

Date: 07/23/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Pilot/Flying J Logistic Services

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that the Pilot/Flying J truck was heading West on US 40 near mm 83 when it was involved in a near head on collision with water truck. The accident resulted in the release of ~100 gallons of diesel fuel from the saddle tanks on the truck and about 10 gallons of DEF. Release was reported to have only been to road surface, and was addressed by emergency response personnel. There was not report of impact to surface waters.
