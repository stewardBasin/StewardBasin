Report #: 12203

Title: Nine Mile Crude-130 bls

Date: 05/16/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Forland Trucking

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Truck hauling crude left the road on a corner and landed in a dry wash. The tanks burst and crude ran for one mile down the dry wash. Work on clean up was haulted until daylight on 5/17/15. Cleanup is currently underway. Clean up by Hammer Oilfield Service company. Kenyon 435 503-7190
