Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11714

Title: Vernal - Hydrochloric acid spill

Date: 02/14/2014

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Weatherford Fracturing Technology

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a hydrochloric acid spill of approximately 1800 gallons. This took place about 3 miles west of Jensen, Utah. Caller said about 50 yards of the spill was inside the fence, 130 yards was outside of the fence, and 5 yards entered a dry bar ditch. No acid is left on top of the ground and it is no longer smoking and steaming. They believe that once it soaked into the ground, the acid became neutralized and they are not planning to do any clean-up of the impacted area unless advised otherwise.
