Incident Summary

Truck rollover caused release of 4,000 gallons of diesel fuel. Is away from highways. No waterways impacted. Emergency response crew is enroute. Coordinates will be given when determined. 11:04 - PRP rep is onsite Pacific West is contractor enroute to remediate. Spill occurred in a dry draw. No inhibitive weather or rain. Spilled off steep embankment on opposite side from access into the area. Wash is est'd 400 yards across. Access was made from Nine Mile road west of Miton, UT and about six miles beyond paved-unpaved demarcation. Caller advised to notify NRC.
