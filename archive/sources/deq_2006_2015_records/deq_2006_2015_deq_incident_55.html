Incident Summary

LCL tanker was delivering fuel to a small farm type elevated tank on legs and in a field. After completing the delivery, the tank truck pulled away from the tank pad, but the slick muddy base under the wheels allowed the truck to slide sideways and the truck pulled off the standing tanks outlet valve. The driver ran over to the tank and placed his hand over the hole thus limiting the total spill to 174 gallons of diesel fuel. The driver kept the hole plugged until the local FD and others arrived to mitigate. No diesel entered a storm drain or any other state waters. A contractor called in by LCL had a vac truck suck up spilled fuel and also the affected soils have been excavated and will be hauled to the Harmaston Bench solid waste disposal facility. Absorbent pads were also used to mitigate the spill. Darrin Brown of the local health department has inspected the site and monitored the clean-up operations. He will follow-up with a final inspection.
