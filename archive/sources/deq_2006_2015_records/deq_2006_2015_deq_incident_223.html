Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11553

Title: Injection Water Release

Date: 09/16/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that 692 barrels of injection water was released over the course of the day. In jection water is a mix of treated production water and fresh water. The caller stated that their equipment allowed them to determine that the 692 barrels was made up of 512 barrels of fresh water and 180 barrels of treated production water. At approximately 4 am they determined that there was a leak. The located the leak and stopped it. There were actually two leaks and the second leak went unnoticed from 4 am until 5 pm. They were able to shutin the release at 5:30 pm. The water ran down a leased access road and into a small stream which eventually flows into the Pariette Wetlands. The caller did not know how far down the stream the water flowed. The company took six water samples from the stream and plan to take some upstream in the morning to determine background levels. Their current plan is to dam the stream and flush the spill from the source to the dam with fresh water. The water will then be sucked from the dam area and disposed. The dam will eventually be removed and the stream allowed to flow again. The leak occurred near well site number 60-26-8-17. The coordinates of the site are: 40degrees 5minutes 21.59 seconds North, 109 degrees 58minutes 34.86seconds West.
