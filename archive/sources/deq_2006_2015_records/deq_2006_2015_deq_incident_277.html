Incident Summary

An excavator struck as underground 3/4 - inch service line next to a home causing natural gas to release into the atmosphere. Due to the release, the house exploded. Response: Four people from the home were sent to the hospital with minor injuries and have since been released. Fire, Police, and State OPS were notified. The gas line was shut off and secured.
