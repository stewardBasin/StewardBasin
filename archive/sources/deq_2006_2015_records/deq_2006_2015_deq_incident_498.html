Title: Uintah Co - Diesel Spill 50 Gallons

Date: 03/12/2014

County: Uintah

Company: Simplot Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.599471576278, -109.465999518714

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting the release of a (rough) estimate volume of 50 gallons of diesel to soils from a vehicle used in the mining process to mix diesel with ammonium nitrate. A check-valve leaked to soils. The entire work area is contained within earthen dam. Will clean up and process through facility. Facility will plans to make a more precise determination of the amount released. No surface waters or drains were impacted.
