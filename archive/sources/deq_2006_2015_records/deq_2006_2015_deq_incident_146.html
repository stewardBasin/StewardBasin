Incident Summary

Caller reported a production water spill of approximately 200 barrels onto the ground and into a canyon that leads to the Green River. Release has not been secured. This incident was reported to DERR by the National Response Center (INR # 1069432) UPDATE (12/26/13, 11:43, KV): The RP, Mark Peak, contacted the NRC a second time to update the initial report. Quantity has increased to 250 barrels. Incident occurred earlier on 12/25/13 at 13:00, not 16:00. No waterways have been impacted. The updated report is NRC INR # 1069471.
