Incident Summary

Vehicle hit a power pole resulting in a power transformer breaking open and spilling 30 gallons of mineral oil to the asphalt, concrete and some down a storm drain. There is no water in the storm drain and is frozen. A cleanup crew is on-site and cleaning up the spill. The transformer is certified as having less than 1 ppm PCBs.
