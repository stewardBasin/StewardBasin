Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil spill

Date: 10/12/2014

County: Uintah

Company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.599471576278, -109.465999518714

Incident Summary

Caller stated a hydraulic line blew out that runs a water pump on a water truck causing 40 gallons of hydraulic oil to spill onto dirt. No waterways were impacted. The contaminated dirt was scraped up with a blade and is being stored for proper disposal.
