Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_478

Title: Production Water Release - Monarch

Date: 10/20/2012

County: Duchesne

Company: E&P Energy

Nearest City: MONARCH

Coordinates: 40.396828923279, -110.066637518615

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of production water (salt water) into irrigation pond through a catch basin at the site. Sheen was observed on the pond and the caller reported that they were unable to obtain an accurate volume but estimated approximately 1 gallon of oily sheen observed on the water surface. The called reported that the water in the pond discharged into an irrigation ditch which dissipates approximately 1000 feet downgradient of the pond. Absorbent booms were placed at the discharge of the pond and along the irrigation ditch to absorb the oily sheen. The caller notified NRC and I provided contact numbers for the TriCounty Health Department.
