Incident Summary

Caller reported that a hose being used to transfer sulfuric acid from a truck broke and spilled approximately 50 gallons of sulfuric acid. The spill has been contained to the site. No water bodies are threatened. They are using soda ash to neutralize the acid. Company Environmental Engineer, John Spencer (435-781-3348) has been contacted and he will supervise the clean up.
