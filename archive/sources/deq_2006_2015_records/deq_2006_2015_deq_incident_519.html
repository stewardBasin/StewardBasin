Incident Summary

Tank truck delivered gas condensate ("60 gravity") to separation station tank at salt water disposal facility. Operator closed valve and uncoupled effluent line from truck. Kerr-McGee crew discovered that valve had not been closed 100% and secondary check valve failed enabling content to slowly drain. Loss estimated at 40 barrels (42 gal/bbl). Estimate 20-30 barrels left the location and flowed about to ephemeral stream channel 400 yards and stopped. Channel is dry so condensate did not mix with any running water. Caller dispatched a backhoe to construct secondary containment downstream of material as preventative measure in the event material is mobilized. Will be checking log to determine attribtution. Contaminated soils will be excavated and disposed at approved landfill. Location is est. 47 miles south of Vernal. White River is the downstream receptor. UPDATE 4/27/07 DEQ DE reports that cleanup by Kerr McGee is going well. Release was caused by faulty valve. Agency representatives will return to perform a post-cleanup survey.
