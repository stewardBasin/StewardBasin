Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 35 gallons non-PCB mineral oil from 2 transformers. The transformers were mounted on a utility pole. The pole had apparently rotted and strong overnight winds knocked the pole and the transformers down onto an asphalt parking area. UP&L crews arrived on-scene to clean-up the released mineral oil.
