Incident Summary

Caller is reporting that there was a release from an injection line near the GBU 13-27-8-17 injection well. ~70 bbls of the treated water left the site and went down a dry drainage. Workers were able to berm the drainage and recover ~55bbls of the water. Line was secured and the drainage will be flushed with approximately 250 bbls of fresh water which will be recovered. Soils samples to be collected next week for submittion to LHD.
