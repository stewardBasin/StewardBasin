Incident Summary

Caller is reporting petroleum contaminated soils discovered during construction excavation near the airport fueling facility. The excavation is only 3-feet deep and impacts to groundwater is unknown. There are 2 above ground storage tanks, AV gas and jet fuel, nearby. The contamination appears to be an old release and weathered. They have contacted the Roosevelt City Manager, (Justin Johnson, 435-7220-5001) to inform them of the contaminated soil. They are in the process of hiring a consultant to come collect samples and determine their next steps...
