Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 04/22/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfiled Exploration & Production Company

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.046844771273, -110.0378073005

Incident Summary

Well site location 12-8H-9-17. Underground injection line broke and treated injection water flowed to nearby dry drainage. Estimated 55 barrels was released off the well pad to the dry drainage eventually reaching a stockwater pond. Flushed with 520 barrels of fresh water.
