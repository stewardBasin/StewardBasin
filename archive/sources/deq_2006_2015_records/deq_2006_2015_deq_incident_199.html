Incident Summary

Diesel truck accident cause more than 20 gallons of diesel fuel to leak onto the road and surrounding soil. The Duch. Fire Dept. crew dammed the area to keep fuel from spreading. Mike Lefler/Fire Marshall and crew retrieved 15 to 20 gallons of fuel in haz mat buckets and gave it to Tracy Williams. The remaining buckets filled with soil were taken by Lefler in the HazMat truck back to Duchesne.
