Incident Summary

UDOT was excavating to install foundation for traffic signal. They encountered an underground storage tank at three feet, ground water at five feet. Excavated to twelve feet. Crew pulled off. Soils and ground water appear contaminated with diesel-like odor. Municipality informed them that there at one time there was a gas station on each corner of this intersection. Tank sits along UDOT Right-of-Way and extends beneath private property of a hotel. UDOT anticipates the circumstance will extend the project time and therefore may pull off of this job at this time. DWQ and ERR Tanks will contact UDOT this evening.
