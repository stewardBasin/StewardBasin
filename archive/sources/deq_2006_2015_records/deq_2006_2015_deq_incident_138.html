Report #: 11571

Title: Diesel spill in Uintah County

Date: 10/04/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: unknown

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 25-gallon diesel fuel spill into a "bar" ditch located on the side of the road. It was contained in the ditch and did not impact any waterways. The clean-up crew were able to retrieve about 20 gallons and will dig up the impacted soil on Monday after Bluestakes surveys the area for underground utilities. Until then, they will cover the impacted soil with a tarp. Caller said the location of this incident is Section 24, T 8 S, R 17 E in either Duchesne County or Uintah County. Caller contacted the BLM and DOGM about this incident. The location pinpointed on attached map is approximate.
