Title: Newfield Ethylene Glycol Spill - Myton

Date: 11/16/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.058399136907, -109.967143475648

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of 1600 gallons of 50/50 ethylene glycol/deionized water mix. The seal on the antifreeze line in the heat tracer through the pump jack engine leaked. Mechanical failure caused the release to surrounding soils. The Host Location is #9-2-9-17, Well # S-2-9-17. 20-acre well. Vacuum trucks were mobilized to remove standing liquids but surrounding soils are saturated. They are waiting for Richard Powell with DOGM to come out and provide a recommendation to excavate saturated soils or provide other response options. Caller reported to Scott Hacking, Richard Powell with UDOGM, and Jim Davis at SITLA.
