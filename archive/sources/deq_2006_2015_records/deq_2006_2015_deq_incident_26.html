Title: Hydrochloric Acid 28% Mix at Commercial Yard

Date: 07/04/2013

County: Uintah

Company: Baker-Hughes Oil Field Operations

Nearest City: VERNAL

Incident Summary

Caller (who was notified at 8:38) reports a hydrochloric acid 28% mixture from a hole in a transport. Volume of release estimated at 10-18 barrels to dirt, asphalt and concrete. Site crew deployed soda-ash. Environmental cleanup contractor out of Salt Lake, Environmental Restoration, has been notified. ETA 3 hours. No impact to surface water or sewers.
