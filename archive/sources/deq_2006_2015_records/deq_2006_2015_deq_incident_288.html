Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_288

Title: Rabbit Gulch Produced Water Release

Date: 08/29/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: E.P. Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.194807938036, -110.546387943445

Incident Summary

NRC Report; CALLER IS REPORTING THAT A POLY FLEX SALT WATER DISPOSAL LINE AT A WELL HEAD FAILED RESULTING IN A SPILL. IT IS UNKNOWN AT THIS TIME IF THERE WAS ANY OIL PRODUCT INVOLVED IN THE SPILL. DUE TO RAIN THE MATERIAL COULD HAVE ENTERED RABBIT GULCH WHICH LEADS TO STARVATION RESERVOIR. DEQ Callin; Spill of produced water with potential of having oil although an oil sheen has not been seen, at this point. Due to rains the spill discharged towards Rabbet Gulch which then leads to Starvation Reservoir. The spill is no longer occurring. No surface water was impacted.
