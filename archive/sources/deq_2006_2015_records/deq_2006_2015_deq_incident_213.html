Incident Summary

Reporting party reports the release of 130 barrels of previously treated injection water to dry drainage. Well location is 13-28-8-17. Sec 28 T8S R17E (coordinates provided 40º 05' 01.10"N 110º 01' 02.98"W). Drainage leads to wildlife stock pond (frozen at this time). Release did not reach the pond. PRP flushed with 600 barrels of fresh water (believed to be of sufficient quantity to carry downstream to pond). Will collect post treatment samples and submit to Tri County HD with cleanup report.
