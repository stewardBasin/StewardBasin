Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11599

Title: EP Energy - 1500 bbls Production Water

Date: 10/26/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy E&P Co., LLC

Nearest City: TALMAGE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of produced water and oil from a 4-inch steel Salt Water Disposal line. The release occurred approximately 9550 feet of the Jensen 2-29A5 drill site. A total of 1500 bbls of produced water and 5 bbls of oil were released during the incident. An estimated 5 gallons of oil and 1500 gallons of produced water flowed into a nearby catch basin used for livestock watering. Landowner was notified of the release and a fence was erected around the basin to prevent livestock access. Released oil becomes a waxy substance when exposed to current temperatures. 1300 bbls of produced water was recovered. Line repaired and fresh water was used to flush the line. On-site contact is Joe Sager (435) 454-4245.
