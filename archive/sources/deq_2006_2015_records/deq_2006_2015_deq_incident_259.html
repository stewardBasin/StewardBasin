Incident Summary

Caller reported a release from the Rust 1-4B3 SWD Transfer station and noticed water running out of the valve set building on the 8 inch line. An estimated 130 bbls of produced water traveled 10’ x 686’ long northeast out of the valve set into a grass field. The release entered a small irrigation ditch with some free standing irrigation water then fanned out into the field. The release occurred due to a cracked 0.5-inch nipple under a pressure transducer on the 8-inch line. It appears that vibration from the 3-inch discharge line leaving the SWD Transfer station caused the nipple to crack, the nipple has been removed and the pressure transducer was left out of service for tonight. Approximately 3 bbls of produced water was recovered from puddles in the field. A clean-up of the visually impacted areas (i.e. iron sulfide) will be performed and removed from the site. No sheen was observed on the waterway.
