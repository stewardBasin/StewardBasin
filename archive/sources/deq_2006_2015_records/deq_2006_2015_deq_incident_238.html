Title: EP Energy, Brine Release,

Date: 06/10/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: MT EMMONS

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Late Wednesday evening a call came in from a land owner that he had found a salt water leak on his property, the night lease operator and production supervisor met with the land owner and went to the location and found the salt water line south of the Brotherson 2-15B4 had developed a leak 2000 feet south of the location. The leak was in the crossing of a dry wash, the water ran 150 yards down the wash and was contained in a catch basin. The pipe line is a 2 7/8 steel line wrapped with black tape and fiberglass lined. The line was exposed and found to have external corrosion. The line will be repaired and hydro tested. Calculator 5 and 6 was used. 90 bbls was spilled Line break spill point GPS: 557199E; 4461018N Wash berm or end point: 557297E; 4461049N (berm or dam constructed by surface owner to catch rainwater) Photos of spill indicate backwash fluid level was several feet above the bottom of the wash some thirty feet west of the line break or spill, which leaves me to believe the volume of water is much higher than reported. The landowner told the operator there was water already in the bermed area below from the rains and so EP Energy reduced their original report of 300 barrels to 90. I believe the operator did recover three loads of spill or spill/rainwater from the bermed area below which was between 240 to 300 barrels of fluid. There was no trace of oil in the spill area. When Scott Hacking and I returned on a second visit the bermed area still had six inches of fluid or more, which either was left or was water that had drained from the saturated soils of the wash. The landowner was contacted and EP Energy promised to send DOGM a contact number. The operator explained that the landowner claimed a spring was present in that wash, but I could not find a (for sure) stream flow at any point and would have to make another trip after the wash drys out.
