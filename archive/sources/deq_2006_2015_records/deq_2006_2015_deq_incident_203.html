Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_203

Title: Duchesne County Treated Water Leak

Date: 07/25/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.045833179062, -110.063892789028

Incident Summary

The caller reported that sometime in the morning, he did not know the exact time, a treated water line leaked just over 300 barrels of water into a dry drainage. The water is a mixture of 70% fresh water and 30% treated water. The caller stated that the dry wash has two small springs that appear and disappear. He was not sure if he should classify the leak is contacting surface or ground water or neither. The leak occurred near well site 9-12-9-16. The GPS coordinates of the actual leak site are: 40 degrees, 2 minutes, 45 seconds North, 110 degrees, 3 minutes, 50 seconds West. The line had been isolated and clean up was underway. The plan was to clean up the spill by flushing with clean water, sucking up with a vacuum truck then flushing the wash with clean water unless directed to do something different.
