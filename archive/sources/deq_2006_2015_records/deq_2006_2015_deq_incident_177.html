Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_177

Title: Uintah Co - Diesel Spill White River 4 gallons

Date: 12/16/2015

County: Uintah

Company: Kerr McGee Oil and Gas Onshore LP

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.041934531808, -109.456334064872

Incident Summary

Caller reported via NRC report a release of diesel fuel into the White River from a pump due to equipment failure. Volume provided is four gallons. Area extent of sheen was reported as 20 ft x 20 ft. Boom was deployed and a VAC truck was dispatched to vacuum visible sheen. Diesel was caught in a zone of backwater flow of an eddy and ice. Caller reported that none of the fuel was carried downstream.
