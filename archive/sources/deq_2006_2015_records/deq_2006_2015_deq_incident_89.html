Title: THIS IS A DRILL...Crude oil spill threatening Lake Fort River in Duchesne County

Date: 06/13/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.225631020927, -110.49877917285

Incident Summary

THIS IS A DRILL....Caller reported that a blow-out occurred at a well site due to overpressurization. Crude oil has entered an unnamed dry drainage area that is located 1/2-mile from Lake Fort River. A clean-up contractor has been contacted to place booms in the drainage area but the equipment is not expected to arrive in time before the oil reaches the river. The quantity of crude oil released is unknown. One injury and subsequent hospitalization was reported. The release has not been secured at this time. THIS IS A DRILL.
