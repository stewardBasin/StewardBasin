Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12016

Title: Produced water spill near Myton

Date: 11/06/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Township 8S Range 16 E Section 24. About 100 barrels of produced water was released from a pipe leak and ran about 200 feet into a dry wash. A contractor will wash the affected area with clean water and sample the soil.
