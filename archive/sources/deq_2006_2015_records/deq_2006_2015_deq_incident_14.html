Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_14

Title: Corrosion Inhibitor Spill

Date: 10/04/2013

County: Uintah

Company: Baker Hughes

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.019952894299, -109.384366993851

Incident Summary

About 330 gallons of a corrosion inhibitor was spilled when a tote was broken. The material is 30 percent methanol and was discharged into a tributary of the White River near Seven Sisters Road 0.5 miles south of Fiddler Road. The district health department has been notified. Their spill report with the MSDS of the material are attached. The location map has been corrected and verified. Coordiantes given are 40.01996, -109.38436.
