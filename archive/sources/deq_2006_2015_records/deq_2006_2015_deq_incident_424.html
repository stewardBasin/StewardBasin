Title: Duchesne County - Process Breakdown

Date: 09/25/2015

County: Duchesne

Incident Summary

Caller reported a process breakdown involving an over pressurized PSV valve on the flow line to a well named Gallaway 1-18B1 approximately 1.5- to 2-miles west of Roosevelt. The release resulted in a flash fire. Local fire department was contacted and extinguished any remnants from the fire.
