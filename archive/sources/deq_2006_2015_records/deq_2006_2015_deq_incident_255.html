Report #: 7098

Title: Simplot Phosphate (Null RP Substituted)

Date: 09/07/2008

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphate

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a hydraulic line on a bull dozer broke releasing 39 gallons of hydraulic oil to the soils at the Simplot facility. A vac-pump was used to recover pooled oil. The contaminated soils were excavated and run through the ore processing system. No storm drains or surface waters were impacted by the release. Swing shift supervisor was unaware of the notification requirements at the time of spill causing the delay in reporting. A written follow-up report will be completed for this incident.
