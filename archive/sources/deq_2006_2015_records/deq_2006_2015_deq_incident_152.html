Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11382

Title: Crude oil spill in Duchesne County

Date: 06/16/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Hosco Trucking

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a super tanker picked up a load of crude oil, then rolled the tanker on a hill releasing approximately 60-100 barrels of oil to a dry drainage area. The main trailer did not leak but the pup trailer did. The oil ran down the dry drainage area about 1/8 to 1/4-mile. The drainage area was dammed off which stopped the spread of oil from going any further. No waterways were impacted. No injuries were reported. Hammer Oilfield Service Company was called for clean-up. They are planning to take the contaminated soil to Environmental Energy Innovations on Nine Mile Road in Duchesne County. Caller said clean-up will be completed tomorrow.
