Incident Summary

A lead container with Cobalt 60 was discovered by a relative of the PRP during a yard sale (no information about how content was determined). The relative notified Uintah County Fire District Director. Readings collected by TRI County environmmental health personnel were reported at 5.03 c/m and 4.10 millirem standing next to the container. Per information received Utah DPS Hazardous Materials personnel are responding 9/27/2011 to secure the material for appropriate disposition.
