Incident Summary

Caller is reporting discovery of visible soil and groundwater contamination after an in-ground oil/water separator was removed for plant shutdown. Contamination consists of miscellaneous oil and grease, etc. PRP will set pump & treat. Soil and groundwater samples have been collected. UPDATE 08/31/11 - This release originated from a regulated underground storage tank program (UST) site. The UST program will oversee cleanup activities.
