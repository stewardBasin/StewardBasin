Incident Summary

Malry McKeachnie noticed someone driving a county truck drumping production water onto his property. He contacted DWR with his complaint, Clint Sampson with DWR contacted Darrin Brown with TCHD at approx 4:30 pm 1/10/11. TCHD visited site at 5:00 pm 1/10/11 spill was identified as production water and samples were collected. Approx 1000 gallons of production water was dumped along roadside, the driver stated it came from Ashley Oilfield. The production water was dumped in a waterway that leads to Ashley Creek waterway is currently frozen. Oil in the production water solidified within 100 yards and is on top of the ice. The county is cleaning up the oil today. NRC was contacted by DEQ at 13:50 1/11/11.
