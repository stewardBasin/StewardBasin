Incident Summary

Spill report from EP Energy on pipeline from the Brotherson 3-23B4 to or past the Brotherson 2-23B4 WDW well into the central battery east of that site. This line has been out of service for a few years and was tested or flushed yesterday with production water before moving Nabors Rig 561 in for a clean out and squeeze job, hoping to put this well back on production. The line leaked in two places, one on location of the 2-23B4 WDW well and the other just south of that site along the pipeline right-of-way just west of the county road. The first spill had been cleaned up before inspection this morning and the second one had not. EP Energy estimated a 5 barrel spill at each leak site for a total of ten barrels. The second release or leak site begins at GPS 559244E; 4460611N and runs north down slope to 559272E; 4460659N. Or about 144'. Photos were taken. Brent Brotherson owns the surface and the second spill might have left the right-or-way by ten or twenty feet. EP Energy was instructed to contact Brent Brotherson regarding the spill.
