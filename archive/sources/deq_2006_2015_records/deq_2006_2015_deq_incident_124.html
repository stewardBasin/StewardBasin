Incident Summary

EP Energy reported a leak in Chevron Pipeline on a western slope that drops into a drainage to the west. Volume released is estimated at 400-500 barrels of production water with a black staining color associated with production water and pipelines. Point of release is on Tribal land but drains westerly into a dry irrigation ditch that is privately owned. EP Energy contacted the land owner and the Tribe. Private owner holds a 16 foot right-of-way along the irrigation ditch corridor. A flowing creek is just down canyon to the west. None of the spill appears to have reached surface water at this time. Production water entered the dry irrigation ditch at (568367E, 4467530N). End of the spill down gradient in the dtich is (5685763E, 4467070N). Spill flowed 2,165 feet per GPS data. A dent was found in the pipeline. EP Energy will investigate to see if the weak point in this line correlates to "smart pig" log data from previous runs. EP Energy plans to flush out the irringation ditch with hot water and evaluate.
