Incident Summary

Caller reported a third-party vacuum truck released approximately 5 gallons of oily waste water onto a concrete containment area. A small portion flowed into the on-site storm drain system that collects in a containment pond. Haliburton responded and contained the release in the pond and will utilize their own vacuum truck to remove the oily waste water. Cleanup is currently underway. 08142012@19:40: Received a call from Brian Hanui representing Thermofluids to report incident.
