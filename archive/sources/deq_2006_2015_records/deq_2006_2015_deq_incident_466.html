Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_466

Title: Injection water spill in Duchesne County

Date: 09/19/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.090998163485, -110.079685185197

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 30 barrel spill of injection water (described as a mix of produced water and fresh water) that flowed towards an ephemeral drainage area. Only 10 barrels actually entered the drainage area. No other waterways were impacted. The injection water soaked into the ground. Caller said there were no plans for clean-up. This incident took place on BLM land. The incident location on the attached map is approximate. UPDATE (9/20/13, 17:35, KV): Tim Eaton of Newfield Exploration called to report a break in a line that happened last night causing an additional 360 barrels of injection water to spill into the dry drainage area. He took samples for lab analysis and will have the results next week. Some of the water was retrieved with a vacuum truck. Caller said that he would flood the impacted area with clean water to dilute the spill if that is required.
