Incident Summary

Reported release of 10-20 gallons from fuel tank on a truck. Truck was parked outside a residence at 240 North 1100 West in Vernal, Utah when release occurred. Accidental turning of a switch on the truck caused the release. Diesel entered a nearby storm drain and ran about two blocks in sewer line. A small amount of water was in sewer at time of release. Owner of truck could not be contacted. Spill reported to Tri-County Health Department by Josh Curtis (435) 789-8496.
