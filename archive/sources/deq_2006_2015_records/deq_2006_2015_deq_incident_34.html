Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11489

Title: Production Water Spill in Duchesne County

Date: 07/30/2013

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Approximately 30 barrels of a mixture of production water/fresh water was released into a dry drainage ditch near Myton. Caller said that of that 30 barrels, only about 5 barrels was filtered production water. The spill ran down the ditch towards the Green River but did not impact any waterways. RP proposes to flush the drainage ditch with fresh water to further dilute the production water but will coordinate with BLM (property owner) on this cleanup option.
