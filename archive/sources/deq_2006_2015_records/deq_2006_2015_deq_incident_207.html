Title: Myton Injection Water Release Newfield

Date: 05/20/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.043891487622, -110.055549580723

Incident Summary

Release from an injection well of treated injection water released 90 bbls to a dry drainage near myton. Internal corrosion leaked water to containment onsite, then line failed and burst under pressure. containment then failed and released 90 bbls to the ground and a dry drainage.Line secured and repairs started. No water to be recovered.
