Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_233

Title: New Field - Release of Production Water

Date: 10/26/2013

County: Duchesne

Company: New Field

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.045615161895, -110.049289340086

Incident Summary

Caller reported that an injection into a water line leaked into a dry drainage. 45 bbls of production water and 200 bbls fresh water were released during the incident and traveled down the dry drainage a distance of 6900 feet. Water samples were pulled from the released material. Line will be repaired and flushed with clean water. Release occurred on BLM land. Incident reported to the DOGM and BLM by the responsible party. UPDATE 10/30/13 11:41: Dave Ingraham (BLM) investigated the spill and reported that there was no indication of oil released at the site. Tim Eaton (New Field) clarified that the report should state 45 bbls production water and 200 bbls of fresh water were released. No oil was released during the incident as initially reported.
