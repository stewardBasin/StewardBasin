Title: Duchesne County - Production Water

Date: 11/15/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: TALMAGE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a flow line developed a leak releasing approximately 337 barrels of production water. No oil has been observed in the production water. The release left the pad at well site Miles 2-1B5 and entered a ditch on private property. Property owner has been notified. The release will be assessed to determine recovery and sampling effort.
