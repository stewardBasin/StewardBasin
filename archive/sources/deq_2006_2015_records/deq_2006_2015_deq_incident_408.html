Report #: 11774

Title: Production Water Spill

Date: 03/31/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Bill Barrett Corp.

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

This spill was reported to DERR via email from Scott Hacking. The spill was originally reported to the Utah Division of Oil, Gas and Mining (DOGM) on Monday, March 31, 2014 by Scott Ghan with Bill Barrett Corp. Scott Ghan reported that a pipeline break surfaced leaving the UDWR 14-29-36 BTR well to the west, approximately sixty to seventy yards down that pipeline and then ran down across the access road and then north into a winter range field owned by the Division of Wildlife Resources (DWR). Scott Gahn estimated they lost more than a hundred barrels of production water. Besides DOGM, he notified the DWR and since the minerals are Tribal, the Tribe was also notified. Because of DOGM's hundred barrel or major event rule, Scott Gahn notified DOGM immediately about this spill event. Scott Hacking investigated the spill site today at around 1 PM. The pipeline break had been fixed and the repair trench was still open. There was a cleanup crew on-site (Chapman Construction) that was removing the contaminated soils. The production water ran an estimated 300 feet north into the nearby rangeland (owned by DWR) , and two containment berms/excavations had been constructed at the terminus. No surface water was impacted. LTE Environmental will collect soil confirmation samples at the cleanup site. Cleanup is well underway.
