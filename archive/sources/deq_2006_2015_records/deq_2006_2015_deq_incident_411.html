Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12239

Title: Myton - Newfield Injection Water

Date: 06/18/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a leak in a treated injection line resulted in the release of 76 bbls of treated injection line water into a dry wash. Release ran an estimated 800 feet down the drainage before the line was isolated and the release was stopped. The dry wash will be flushed with fresh water and the soils sampled to determine if further remediation is warranted. Release is on BLM property. BLM and DOGM (Richard Powell) were also notified of the release. Newfield Location # 8-14-9-15
