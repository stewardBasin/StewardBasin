Incident Summary

The caller is reporting that black wax crude oil discharged from a tanker pump due to a vehicle rollover. The caller stated that the oil is in a ravine that is classified as a navigable waterway. No water is in the ravine at this time. Tesoro has hired a cleanup contractor who will be removing the oil on February 24 when access to the spill site has been granted by the property owner. Right now the spill is on a farmer's property. The approximate spill location is provided in an attached Google Earth placemark.
