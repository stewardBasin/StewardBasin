Incident Summary

Driver of passenger truck fell asleep at wheel, went off-road and ran into methanol storage tank which ruptured and released ~200 gallons onto gravel outside of the containment area directly onto the well pad. UHP responded to traffic accident that had no injuries (UHP Trooper Swenson w/ Report #050900209). Owner of tank (Rosewood Resources) will have J West Oilfield Services remove impacted soils for disposal at Lapoint Recycle and Storage Facility which is scheduled for 3/18. RP has already contacted BLM which owns the property where the spill occurred.
