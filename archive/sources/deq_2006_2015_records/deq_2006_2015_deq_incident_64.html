Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12289

Title: Duchesne County - Treated water spill

Date: 07/31/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a line leak resulting in a preliminary estimate of 50 barrels of treated water that leaked off of a right-of-way and into a retention pond that contained water. Spill occurred near injection water well #8-5-9-17. Clean-up is underway. The pond was pumped out and will be allowed to dry. Then it will be flushed with fresh water. Scott Hacking, DEQ Engineer, was on scene at the time. Caller also contacted Richard Powell (DOGM) and Randy Bywater (BLM).
