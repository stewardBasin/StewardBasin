Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 11787

Title: Duchesne County Diesel Release

Date: 04/16/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration & Production Co

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a contractor was using a diesel power washer near well site Aubery 1A-15-22-3-2-WH when he observed a malfunction at a nearby centrifuge. During the process the contractor failed to turn off the shutoff valve releasing approximately 6 barrels of diesel fuel. 5 barrels was contained on the tarp within the bermed area at the well site while the remaining 1 barrel was released to soil. The diesel was cleaned up by excavating and mixing with fly ash then placing into a cuttings pit at another location.
