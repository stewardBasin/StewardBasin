Report #: 11914

Title: Diesel spill in Roosevelt

Date: 08/05/2014

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Unknown

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A vandal pulled a fuel line truck on a bulldozer at a construction company resulting in the release of about 50 gallons of diesel fuel to soil. The impacted soil will be excavated and disposed of.
