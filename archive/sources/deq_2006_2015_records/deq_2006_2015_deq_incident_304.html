Title: Savage crude oil/diesel

Date: 11/28/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Savage Trucking

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.157178720194, -110.183870241357

Incident Summary

Dispatch called me at 9 am Saturday morning, Nov 28, to report a tanker rollover. They stated that the tanker had lost very little crude, but 100 gallons of diesel. I spoke with trooper Tipman onsite who stated they had lost at least 75 gallons of diesel. I spoke with Savage on Sunday and asked them to report the spill. They have not, so I am reporting on Dec 1, 2015. I have no information on actions taken.
