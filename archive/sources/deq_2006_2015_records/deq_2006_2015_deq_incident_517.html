County: Duchesne

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of approximately 75 barrels of treated injection water. Water origniated from right-of-way to off-location and spread out into an area of large open range for about 500 yards. No surface drainages were impacted. Caused by internal corrosion of the service line. Crew has isolated the line and repairing. PRP is determining course of remedial action.
