Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: deq_incident_240

Title: Mine Reclaim Water Release

Date: 09/21/1996

County: Uintah

Company: S.F. Phosphates Limited Co.

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.60273915184, -109.491111225389

Incident Summary

Spill of approx. 12,000 gallons of reclaim water: Did not leave property. Spill location is approx. 11 miles north of Vernal. large 30 year old tailings pond is constructed with an earthen dam with know seepage. Series of catch basins etc. are in place to recover all seepage and transfer it into a catch pond, line pumping back into tailings pond broke. Flow migrated toward fresh water stream Brush Creek. Flow stopped about 75 yards short of the creek. Water is also used to irrigate hay. Water used in flotation circuit for phosphate. Includes minute amounts of flotation reagents including diesel, west coast fatty acid ("WCFA"), petroleum sulfonate (1840-X, manufactured by Witco) and a frother which is an alcohol based synthetic. This mixture is added at a rate measured in cc/min into 30,000 gal/min of water. Water is maintained at range of 7.2 - 7.5. Leroy checked the flow path to verify that no water entered the creek.
