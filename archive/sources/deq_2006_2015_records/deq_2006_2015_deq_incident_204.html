Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of approximately 15 barrels of treated injection water. Internal corrosion caused a crack of a line resulting in a release of treated injection water that ran 100 yards down a dry drainage. The drainage was flushed with clean water and sampling and remediation are taking place. The line was isolated and is out of service until repairs can be made and get the line back in service in a few days. The Well number is 11-28-8-17 The well location is not confirmed and the map location is not accurate. Newfield will call with well coordinates Monday morning. Ryan has already contacted Richard Powell at DOGM and Randy Bywater at BLM.
