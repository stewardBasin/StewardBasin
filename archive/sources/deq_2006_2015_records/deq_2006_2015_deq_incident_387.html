Report #: 12271

Title: EP Energy 80 bbl production water

Date: 07/16/2015

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: ALTAMONT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Leak in water disposal line along pipeline right of way leaving the Brotherson 2-14B4 well north along access road approximately 300 feet, GPS location 558896E; 4462270N. Spill ran nearly half a mile east along a dry, sandy wash , then spilled into a drainage before entering a shallow pond where water collects along a right of way road before continuing on into Pigeon Water Creek. Water samples taken by Baker Hughs and witnessed by DOGM inspector: spill source, 1st pond (was dry with downhill berm washed out), west and east side of pond near road.
