Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12325

Title: Uinta County - Condensate release

Date: 05/20/2015

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Foundation Energy

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a tank overflow at the Wolf Point Compressor Station resulted in the release of an estimated 2 bbls. of production water to the soils. Release occurred on 5/20/15 but was not reported until 8/20/15. At the time of the release a vac-truck was used to collect the release where it had pooled on the ground. Release occurred on-site but subsequent heavy rains washed the material 100 yards off-site. Release occurred on SITLA lands. Location given in this report for the release is approximated and based off the nearby SE Flank Uintah 1-28 well. Caller reported no surface waters were impacted. Notification was made on 8/20/15 to Scott Hacking (UDEQ), Jim Davis (SITLA), Richard Powell (DOGM). UDEQ and DOGM visited the site and determined the release to be greater than 2 bbls.
