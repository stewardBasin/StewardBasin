Title: Duchesne County - Injection water spill

Date: 11/17/2015

County: Duchesne

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.069437500694, -110.152775262613

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 286 barrels of treated injection water. Of those, 197 barrels stayed on location and 89 barrels went off location into a dry drainage ditch. They were able to recover 175 barrels. Clean-up is underway. They will flush the area with fresh water and then take soil samples for lab analysis. Caller said that he contacted Scott Hacking, DEQ Engineer, Richard Powell with DOGM, and Jim Davis with SITLA.
