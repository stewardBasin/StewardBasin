Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Title: Production Water Release near Ouray

Date: 10/11/2015

County: Uintah

Company: Newfield Energy

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates: 40.039255224126, -110.085198809951

Incident Summary

About 28 barrels of filtered production water were released into a dry wash from a leaking underground line. The area was cleaned and will be flushed. An attempt was made by the company to notify DEQ October 12 but the system did not work properly.
