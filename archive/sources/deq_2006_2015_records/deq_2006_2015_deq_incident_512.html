Title: Natural Gas Release - Uintah Co.

Date: 04/18/2015

County: Uintah

Company: Wonsits Valley Compressor Station

Nearest City: UINTAH

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of 7.66 Million cubic feet of natural gas from a valve that was open overnight. The facility operator left at 10:00 PM Friday night and when a operator returned at 7:00 AM the next morning, they discovered the valve on the contactor tower was open. The valve company bypassed the valve to stop the release and is in the process of repairing the valve.
