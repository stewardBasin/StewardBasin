Title: Roosevelt ITL Production Water Dumping.

Date: 03/15/2014

County: Duchesne

Company: Iowa Tank Lines

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates: 40.334933808296, -110.082018336365

Incident Summary

Forwarding a complaint sent to DOGM by EP Energy from a lady who lives in Duchesne County. The complaint detailed a driver for Iowa Tank Lines truck who was seen and photographed dumping production water into the wash bed on Saturday, March 15th. The complaint has photos of the truck and numbers at the time of the dumping but has not forwarded them as yet; the complainant claims that he dumped water on the road in the past that had oil in it. The spill site is at 0577975E; 4465337N and immediately off the right or east side of the road into the lower of the wash. The wash bottom is fine-grained sand so it is hard to determine how much water was dumped, but it did run down the wash over a hundred feet, a wash that has several low areas that would puddle until the water evaporates or subs away. I will forward another email when I receive the photos and the offenders name and truck information.
