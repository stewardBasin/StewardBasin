Title: Simplot Phosphate Diesel Spill Uintah County

Date: 06/16/2015

County: Uintah

Incident Summary

Fueler was servicing the overburden drill on panel D of the mine when diesel hose ruptured and approximately 100 gallons of diesel was spilled on the ground on top of the ore body.
