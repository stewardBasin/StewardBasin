Incident Summary

Caller reported that an break on a hydraulic line from a service truck released 30 gallons of hydraulic oil to the surrounding soils. Release occurred on BLM lands south of Myton. Service truck was working in the area of a Questar Pipeline installation when the release occurred. Contaminated soils were removed and the PRP will conduct a final assessment of the release later this week.
