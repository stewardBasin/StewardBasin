Duchesne - Release of crude oil

DEQ Report #: 14314

Date: 02/22/2020

County: UINTAH

Nearest City: FORT DUCHESNE

Responsible Party: Pilot Crude Hauling

Source status: Steward Basin local archive page generated from DEQ incident JSON record.

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a valve on a truck was not properly closed resulting in the release of 3 barrels (bbls) of crude oil to the caliche soils. Release occurred at the loading facility at a tank battery south of Fort Duchesne. No waterways were impacted. Envirocare responded to the release on 2/22/2020 and shoveled the crude oil into drums.
