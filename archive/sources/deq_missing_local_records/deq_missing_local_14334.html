Incident Summary

Caller reported a 4-barrel treated injection water release after an underground steel pipeline developed a leak. The water surfaced on a right-of-way and split with half going down the side of a road and the other half going to a dry drainage ditch. No waterways were impacted. Clean-up is underway.
