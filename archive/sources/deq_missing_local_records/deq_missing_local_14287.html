Myton - Produced water release

DEQ Report #: 14287

Date: 01/28/2020

County: DUCHESNE

Nearest City: MYTON

Responsible Party: Newfield Exploration

Source status: Steward Basin local archive page generated from DEQ incident JSON record.

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 9 barrels (bbls) of produced water from a 2 inch steel surface line. A reported 4 bbls of the produced water ran off the site into a nearby flat area. The release occurred when water left in the line froze, causing a breakage in the line. The property owners whose land was impacted by the release, Jason Riley (Roosevelt) and Codey Aroches (Roosevelt), were notified by Newfield Exploration. No drainages or surface waters were impacted by the release.
