Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of 34 gallons of compressor oil that sprayed out when the drill quit working. The oil usually drains back into the machine, but when it died, the oil sprayed out. Impacts are only to the drill bench and no waterways impacted and no injuries. Impacted soils will be excavated and disposed of by an outside contractor when the drill is moved.
