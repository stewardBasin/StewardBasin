Vernal - Well water contaminated

DEQ Report #: 14299

Date: 02/10/2020

County: UINTAH

Nearest City: VERNAL

Responsible Party: unknown

Source status: Steward Basin local archive page generated from DEQ incident JSON record.

Incident Summary

Caller bought his house recently and checked the condition of his well. He got a sample of well water and discovered that its color was a light neon green and it smelled like fuel. He threw the well water onto some weeds and was able to light it on fire. The well is located approximately 35 meters from his house and his house is about 200 yards from Ashley Creek. Caller said he wants to use the well to water livestock this spring.
