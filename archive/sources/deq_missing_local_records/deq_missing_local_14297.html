Incident Summary

On the morning of Monday 2/10/2020, control was lost when an electric generator was being unloaded from the back of a flatbed truck. The generator contained approximately 200 gallons of diesel fuel, which spilled onto the dirt roadway. No waterways or drains were directly impacted. Environmental Management Inc was contracted to clean up the spill and will commence cleaning on 2/11/2020.
