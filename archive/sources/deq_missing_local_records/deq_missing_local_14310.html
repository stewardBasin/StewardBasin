Myton - Diesel release in Nine Mile Canyon

DEQ Report #: 14310

Date: 02/13/2020

County: DUCHESNE

Nearest City: MYTON

Responsible Party: Hanson Trucking

Source status: Steward Basin local archive page generated from DEQ incident JSON record.

Incident Summary

Caller reported a truck rolled over in Nine Mile Canyon resulting in the release of 80 gallons of diesel from the saddle tanks. Diesel released to the soils on the side of the road. EMB Oil Field Services was contracted to clean up the release. Caller additionally reported they notified Tri-County Health of the release.
