Incident Summary

Caller reported that an underground steel pipeline developed a leak. 4 barrels of treated injection water surfaced and ran off the pipeline right-of-way in to a dry drainage. Pipeline leak was isolated for repairs. Cleanup will proceed in accordance with BLM instructions.
