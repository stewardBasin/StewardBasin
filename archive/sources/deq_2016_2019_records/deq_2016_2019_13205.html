Title: Duschene County -- Produced water spill

Date: 10/22/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy E&P Company, L. P.

Nearest City: BONETA

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A leak developed in a saltwater disposal flex pipe at Freeman 4-16B4 API #43-013-50438. The leak surfaced just outside of the pad berm and ran about 175 feet to the north. The line has been taken out of service. A crew will dig up the line to determine the cause of the leak and the line will be repaired and placed back into service. UT DOGM Incident #4114.
