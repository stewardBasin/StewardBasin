Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12568

Title: Myton - Release of Treated Injection Water

Date: 04/05/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.08027184507, -110.009176579345

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a line leak resulted in the release of 298 bbls of treated injection line water. A reported 255 bbls remained on-site at well location 2-33-8-17. The remaining 43 bbls released off-site into a dry drainage. Crews were able to contain the release and are working to repair the line. BLM (Randy Bywater) and Utah DOGM (Amy Doebele) were notified of the release.
