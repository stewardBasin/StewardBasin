Title: Uintah County -- Produced water release

Date: 10/24/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Finley Resources, Inc.

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Incident Summary

A produced water pipeline developed a leak and released an estimated 1,250 barrels of produced water onto the right of way. The leak was contained within 30 minutes.
