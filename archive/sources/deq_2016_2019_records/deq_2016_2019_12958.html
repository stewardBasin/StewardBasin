Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12958

Title: Unitah Co - Hydraulic Spill

Date: 04/11/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphates, Inc.

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting the release of hydraulic oil to soils from a ruptured line on drill #1822. Oil released to surrounding soils. The crew's initial estimate of the volume was 15 gallons. Repairs were completed this morning and the reservoir was filled taking 71 gallons which prompted the notification. No impact to surface waters or drains. Soils are being excavated for storage and delivery to the county landfill.
