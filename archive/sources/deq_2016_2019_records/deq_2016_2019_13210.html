Title: Uintah County -- Crude oil release

Date: 11/03/2017

County: UINTAH

Company: QEP Energy Company

Nearest City: VERNAL

Incident Summary

The flow line between test batteries WV5 and WV6 began leaking, releasing an estimated 8 barrels of crude oil onto the ground. No waterways were impacted.
