Title: Matt Warner Reservoir -- Suspected Algal Bloom

Incident Summary

DWQ employees collecting samples reported seeing signs of algal bloom developing on the lake. Because of the report and recent history of algal blooms on the reservoir, Tri-County HD issued an algal bloom warning.
