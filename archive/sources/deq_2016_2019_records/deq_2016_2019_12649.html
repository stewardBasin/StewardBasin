Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12649

Title: Uintah Co. - Crude Oil from Tanker Truck

Date: 06/14/2016

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Basin Western Trucking

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that an overfill of a tanker truck released 4.5 bbls of crude oil at a well site XTO 15-17EX. Crude oil remained on the exploration site. No surface waters were impacted. Well head is located on BLM land. Western Basin Trucking will excavate contaminated soils and stage it on plastic sheeting. Soils will then be characterized for proper disposal.
