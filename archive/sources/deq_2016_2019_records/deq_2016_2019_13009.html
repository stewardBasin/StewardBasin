Report #: 13009

Title: Myton - Injection Water Release

Date: 05/31/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported internal corrosion of a injection water pipeline resulted in the release of 35 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water. The release ran down the shoulder of the road, off the right-of-way, and then traveled 95 feet down a dry drainage. Crews isolated the leak, flushed the impacted soils with fresh water, and collected soil samples. Nathan Hall (UDEQ-DE) visited the release site. Release location is Well ID 4-8-9-17 or API 4301332273.
