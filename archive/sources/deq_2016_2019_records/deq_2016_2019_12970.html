Title: Duchesne County Treated Injection Water Leak

Date: 04/16/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.03330727799, -110.0884194005

Incident Summary

The caller reported a leak of 175 barrels of treated injection water at well location 6-14-9-16. The water ran down a dry drainage for approximately 3390 feet. No water bodies are impacted or threatened by the leak. The API number of the well is 4301332337 GPS Coordinates are: 40 degrees north, 1 minute, 59.91 seconds; 110 degrees west, 5 minutes, 18.24 seconds. The leak has been isolated and shut in. Repairs will be made as soon as possible. Soil samples will be taken and analyzed with the results used to determine what remediation is needed.
