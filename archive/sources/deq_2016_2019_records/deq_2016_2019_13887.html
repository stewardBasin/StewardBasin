Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13887

Title: Duchesne County -- Natural gas release

Date: 05/01/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Company reports that an estimated 660 Mcf of natural gas was released from a severed 1" HDPE pipeline in Coyote Canyon after a suspected ATV or automobile strike. Release was discovered by EPA GMAP vehicle in area. UDOGM and BLM have been notified.
