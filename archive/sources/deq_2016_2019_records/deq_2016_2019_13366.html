Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13366

Title: Uintah County -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 04/03/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Coordinates: 40.175167507524, -109.907061945879

Incident Summary

Water contractor was pumping treated effluent water from Roosevelt City to frac location for well injection. LayFlat line may have been rubbing against a rock and split, releasing an estimated 151 barrels of water to the soil. Line split was repaired, restaked to prevent movement, and put back into service. Contractor intends to replace line as soon as possible.
