Title: Produced Water & Oil - Vernal

Date: 06/09/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Middle Fork Energy LLC

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of 9.5 barrels of oil and produced water from an oil well. The polish rod broke releasing the product. The reported location is Red Wash 23-23A, API 4304715228. Most of the product was contained to the pad, but approximately 2 barrels flowed off location down a dry ditch about 300-feet. The leak was shut off and dirt berms were used to control the flow. Cleanup will be complete by tomorrow with shovel removal of the impacted soils in the drainage and power washing the pad and excavating impacted soils. Caller already notified DOGM and Tri County Health Dept.
