Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13695

Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil release at Simplot

Date: 11/29/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Simplot - Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.593620917251, -109.467716804687

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a hydraulic line on a rotary drill rig ruptured releasing an unknown quantity of hydraulic oil (current best guess estimate is 50-75 gallons). Once the line is repaired and the oil reservoir tank is refilled, the released quantity will be determined. The release impacted the overburden soils beneath the drill rig before two small plastic swimming pools could be placed as temporary secondary containment to contain the release.
