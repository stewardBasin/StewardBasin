Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12575

Title: Duchesne County - Injection Water Line

Date: 04/15/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.073047014854, -110.005059454871

Incident Summary

Caller reported a pressure line leak near a failed fitting resulting in a preliminary estimate of 159 barrels of injection water which traveled from the pad into a dry drainage. Spill occurred near well #9-33-8-17 which is BLM land. The water traveled approximately 4,500 feet. The line is being isolated for replacement. Clean-up is underway followed by flushing with fresh water. Caller contacted Amy Dobley (DOGM) and Randy Bywater (BLM). He is also planning to contact Scott Hacking, DEQ Engineer.
