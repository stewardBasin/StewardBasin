Incident Summary

Reporting party states an unknown quantity of crude oil leaked from a well onto the ground and into a nearby pond, possibly affecting vegetation and wildlife. Incident is believed to have occurred in January, 2019 and was not discovered until 2 Apr 19. Report is late due to a breakdown in communication.
