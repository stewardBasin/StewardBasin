Incident Summary

Caller reported a leak in an underground pipeline resulted in the release of 15 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water. The release surfaced and 10 bbls remained on the well site and 5 bbls released off-site into a dry drainage. Line will be repaired. Lat/Long Coordinates 40°2'37.17" North/109°59'40.54" West
