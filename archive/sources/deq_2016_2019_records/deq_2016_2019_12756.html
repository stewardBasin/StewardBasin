Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12756

Title: Injection water spill near Myton

Date: 09/21/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Energy

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Internal pipeline corrosion caused 92 barrel loss of treated injection water. The water flowed 1395 feet down a drainage ditch to a sedimentation basin/cattle pond. The company will pump the pond dry, flush the pond and sample to ensure adequate cleanup.
