Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14042

Title: Duchesne County Produced Water Release

Date: 08/10/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that while pressure testing a line a valve was left open and about 600 barrels of produced water and 3 barrels of slop oil were released onto soil. No water bodies or drains were impacted. The oil and water that did not soak into the ground was contained and has been removed. A contractor is on site for soil removal.
