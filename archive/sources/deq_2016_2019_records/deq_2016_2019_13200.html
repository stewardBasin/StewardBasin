Title: 38 Barrel Injection Water Release near Myton

Date: 10/30/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Production

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.084381638301, -110.025279948011

Incident Summary

About 38 barrels of treated injection water was lost from an underground transfer line to a dry wash. Cleanup should be completed today. DOGM, BLM and the DEQ district engineer were notified by the company.
