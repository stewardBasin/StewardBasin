Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12937

Title: Unitah Co - Hydraulic Spill

Date: 03/14/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphates, Inc.

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Main O-ring on front-end loader failed causing release of hydraulic oil to soils. Contaminated soils were excavated and placed in disposal containment area. Estimated volume of release 70 gallons based on post-repair top-off.
