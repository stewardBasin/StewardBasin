Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12874

Title: Uintah Co. - Produced Water release

Date: 01/18/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: QEP Energy

Nearest City: NAPLES

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 23 barrels of produced water from an injection well location. Well ID RW-41-27B. Produced water was released off-site from the well pad and ran along the pipeline right-of-way. Response crew is on-scene with a vac-truck. Caller made notifications to Vernal BLM and DOGM offices and to the Tri-County Health Department.
