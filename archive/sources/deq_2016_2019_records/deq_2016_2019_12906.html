Title: Injection Water Release, Myton

Date: 02/15/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.024624039603, -110.063807741028

Incident Summary

Newfield reported a 20 barrel leak in a 2 inch injection line due to internal corrosion that ran 540 feet down a dry wash. Clean up is underway. Newfield has reported to DOGM and BLM.
