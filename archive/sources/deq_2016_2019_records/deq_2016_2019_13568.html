Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 08/15/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.085891449573, -110.013130065809

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 29 barrel release of treated injection water from an underground pipeline (Well ID 11-28-8-17) which surfaced and ran into the dry drainage located in a sensitive cactus habitat. Cleanup is on hold until the BLM completes a survey of the area. The spill is contained. Notifications have been made with DOGM, BLM, Tri-County/DEQ DE, and USFWS.
