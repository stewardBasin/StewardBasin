Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13266

Title: UPDATE Duchesne County -- Crude oil mist

Date: 12/21/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: CEDARVIEW

Coordinates: 40.367045893175, -110.001997708922

Incident Summary

A drilling rig experienced a blowout, which created a fine mist of crude oil that fell onto the surface of the pad. A small amount floated down onto the neighboring property. The ground was mostly covered with snow at the time and there was no free-flowing liquid crude oil observed on the surface. No waterways have been impacted. Estimated total of oil is 6 barrels. Crews began cleanup on 22 Dec 17. Report has been updated with accurate location.
