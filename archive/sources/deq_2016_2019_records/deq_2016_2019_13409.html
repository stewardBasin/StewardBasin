Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13409

Title: Recycled Burner Fuel Spill, near Maeser

Date: 05/16/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Burdick Materials

Nearest City: MAESER

Coordinates: 40.442501659587, -109.629707737016

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 70 gallons of recycled burner fuel at an asphalt plant. Material has been bermed and absorbed and will be recycled back through the plant to produce asphalt. No injuries or water impacted. Material stayed within the confines of the site.
