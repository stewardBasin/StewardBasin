Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13055

Title: Duchesne County -- Production Water Spill

Date: 07/11/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E&P Company

Nearest City: TALMAGE

Coordinates: 40.277644841102, -110.431194285905

Incident Summary

Company reports 105 barrels of production water was released from a broken line from the Wilson 3-36B5 well. Water flowed approximately 300 feet down the right of way and then left the ROW and flowed another 260 feet. Spill is near a dry wash.
