Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13067

Title: Uintah County -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 07/14/2017

County: UINTAH

Company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Coordinates: 40.225506985989, -109.859039394195

Incident Summary

Employees of the company were clearing out a pipeline which contained treated sewage effluent from Roosevelt City for use as injection water and an estimated 200 barrels was discharged onto the ground.
