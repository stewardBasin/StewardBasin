Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12573

Title: Roosevelt - Diesel release

Date: 04/12/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Pilot Travel Center

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a customer overfilled his saddle tank resulting in a release of 70 gallons of diesel fiel. Release was mostly contained to the concrete pad however a small amount of diesel reached the adjacent soils. No storm drains or surface waters were impacted. Environmental Restoration was contracted to remediate spill. Absorbant materials, booms and pads were deployed to collect the release. The small amount of impacted soils were hand excavated. Waste materials were containerized into two 55-gallon drums for disposal.
