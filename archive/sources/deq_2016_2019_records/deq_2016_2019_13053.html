Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13053

Title: Roosevelt -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 07/08/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A pipe carrying treated water from Roosevelt City sewage lagoons broke and discharged approximately 200 barrels. The water was intended for use as injection water for drilling. Water flowed onto road and right of way. No waterways impacted, breakage has been repaired.
