Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13277

Title: Duchesne County Injection Water Release

Date: 01/06/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that an underground pipeline developed a leak and released 80 barrels of treated injection water to the surface. The water ran along the side of a road and into a dry drainage. No water bodies were impacted. The coordinates of the leak are: 40 Degrees 2 Minutes 50.1 seconds north 110 Degrees 1 Minute 9.58 seconds west The nearest well site is number 12-9H-9-17. Newfield has shut-in the line and is making repairs to the pipe. Clean up of the impacted soils along the road has begun. Clean up in the dry drainage will be coordinated with BLM and the DEQ District Engineer because it is in an endangered cactus area.
