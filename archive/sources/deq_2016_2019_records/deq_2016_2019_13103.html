Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13103

Title: Uintah County Produced Water Release

Date: 08/13/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: QEP Energy

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that approximately 30 barrels of produced water were released at a water injection well site number WV50. The coordinates of the site are: Latitude: 40.12528, Longitude: -109.53812. The cause of the release was a cow rubbing the handle on a ball valve that opened the valve. The valve has been closed and the handle removed to prevent incidents like this in the future. All water was contained to the site and has been collected and taken to a disposal well. There were no threats to any water bodies.
