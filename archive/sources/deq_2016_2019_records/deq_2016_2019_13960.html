Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13960

Title: Unitah County Diesel Spill

Date: 07/03/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of an estimated 50 gallons of diesel from a fuel truck. The automatic shutoff caused back pressure that popped a line off causing a release. Release was to soils. Contaminated soils were excavating to temporary storage to be disposed to the landfill.
