Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14119

Title: Myton - Treated Injection Water Release

Date: 09/16/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Encana formerly known as NewfeildEnc

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that an underground steel pipeline leaked treated injection water (well #12-33-8-17) which ran down to a dry drainage. An estimated 24 barrels were release. No surface water was impacted. The line was isolated and will be repaired. Encana is waiting on BLM to give the OK to begin clean-up.
