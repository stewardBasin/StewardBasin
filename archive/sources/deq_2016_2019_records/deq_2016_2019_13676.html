Title: Tri-County - Treated Injection Water release

Date: 11/13/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported an underground steel pipeline developed a leak resulting in the release of 19 barrels (bbl) of treated injection water. The release surfaced along the pipeline corridor and ran into a nearby dry drainage. Caller stated the release location was near Injection Well # 8-14-9-15. The pipeline was repaired and remediation will occur in accordance with BLM guidance within the next 7-10 days.
