Incident Summary

Approximately 39 barrels of purge water was release from a salt water disposal line on August 9, 2018 when a pipeline ruptured. The purge water ran northward along the pipeline right-of-way approximately 420 feet while remaining on the property. Utah DOGM Incident #4492.
