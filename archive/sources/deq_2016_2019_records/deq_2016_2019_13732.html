Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13732

Title: Duchesne County -- Produced Water and Glycol Spill

Date: 11/30/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: MT EMMONS

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

An equipment failure caused the release of an estimated 15 barrels of produced water mixed with glycol. Most of the liquid was captured on site but approximately 1 barrel of material flowed off site. Due to confusion about reporting requirements DWQ was not notified of the incident at the time.
