Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13209

Title: Bluebell -- Produced water release from pipeline

Date: 11/11/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.353207509187, -110.196231454027

Incident Summary

Company reported a release of 7 barrels of produced water from the Ute 3-35A3 pipeline near the Ute 1-35A3 battery. The release traveled an estimated 300 feet and soaked into the ground. LT Environmental is in charge of cleanup.
