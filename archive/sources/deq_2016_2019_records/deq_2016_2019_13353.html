Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13353

Title: Uintah County -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 03/15/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Coordinates: 40.220636802635, -109.899432750902

Incident Summary

Water contractor was pumping water which contained treated effluent from Roosevelt City to frac location for well injection. A different contractor was tied in to the line, pre-heating the water. The water contractor shut down the pump but the heating contractor was not aware of the shutdown. Air entered the heater and expanded, causing the connections to fail which resulted in the release of approximately 200 barrels of water onto wellsite area.
