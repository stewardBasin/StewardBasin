Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13842

Title: Duchesne County Treated Injection Water Release

Date: 04/12/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Encana - Formerly Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that a buried, steel pipeline leaked 107 barrels of treated injection water that ran off the well site and into a dry drainage. It flowed down the drainage and into a stock water pond that was full to capacity causing the pond to breach and allowing the injection water and contaminated stock water to flow approximately 5049 additional feet down the dry drainage. The well number is 22-6-9-17. The line has been shut in and isolated. No water bodies besides the stock pond were impacted. Clean up will include removing all contaminated water from the pond and flushing the pond and drainage with approximately 200 barrels of fresh water. Nathan Hall is overseeing the clean up.
