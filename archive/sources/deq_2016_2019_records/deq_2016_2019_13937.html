Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13937

Title: Duchesne County - Injection water release

Date: 06/14/2019

County: UINTAH

Company: Encana Oil & Gas

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.10909837487, -109.975622960177

Incident Summary

Caller reported the discovery of an underground steel pipeline that leaked 60 barrels of treated injection water. It surfaced on a right of way and ran down a dry drainage ditch about 100 yards. The leak was isolated and shut off. Repairs will be made on Monday. The release site will be cleaned up but BLM needs to survey the drainage area before cleanup takes place there because it is a sensitive habitat area.
