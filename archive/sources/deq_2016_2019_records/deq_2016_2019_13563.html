Report #: 13563

Title: Produced Water - Altamont

Date: 08/09/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: ALTAMONT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of 124 barrels of produced water from a field pipeline. The pipeline is a poly with fiberglass wrap and was ruptured from water hammer effects. The release flowed into a culvert in their access road then surfaced in a dry irrigation ditch where it pooled 100-feet from the release location. A vacuum truck removed the standing water and taken to IWM for disposal. Nathan Hall DE was notified on 8/9/18 and Dennis Ingram at UDOGM was also notified on 8/9/18.
