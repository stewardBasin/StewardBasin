Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13488

Title: Produced water release near Bluebell

Date: 07/06/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

An estimated 5,000 barrels of drilling brine with zinc salts spilled from a collection line to a grass field and an irrigation pond. Oil Gas and Mining is on-site. The spill is being pumped up and oil collection booms have been placed at the outlets in case a sheen is identified in the water.
