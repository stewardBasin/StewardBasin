Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13444

Title: Salt water spill in Duchesne County

Date: 06/08/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.303448860918, -110.378919467196

Incident Summary

About 98 barrels of salt water was released to a dry wash from a transmission line. The release traveled about 1550 feet from the release. The area has been isolated and the spill location identified. LT Environmental will collect soil samples.
