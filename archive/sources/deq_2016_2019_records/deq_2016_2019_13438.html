Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13438

Title: Injection water release near Myton

Date: 06/06/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Newfield Production

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates: 40.079163332256, -109.937966320984

Incident Summary

An underground treated injection water line sprung a leak releasing 108 barrels of water to environmentally sensitive, BLM-controlled land. Cleanup will await a BLM survey. Newfield notified BLM and DOGM.
