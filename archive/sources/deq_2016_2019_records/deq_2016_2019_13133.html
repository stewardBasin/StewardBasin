Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 09/01/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.039512585873, -110.084703062085

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 72 bbl release of treated injection water from the 34-11-9-16 well. The leak started in a line and traveled down the road into a dry drainage then down another road where it stopped and puddled up. Approximately 20 bbls were recovered from this location. Cleanup is in progress. The caller notified DOGM, BLM, and the DEQ DE. No waterways were impacted.
