Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12569

Title: Uintah Co - Treated Water

Date: 04/04/2016

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Anadarko

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Liquid transfer line from Sage Grouse tank battery to Goat Pature Evaporation pond ruptured and leaked approximately 400 BBL of produced water. This was water that had been filtered so there was no condensate. The rupture is by a liquid manifold building in the SWSE of section 17 in T10S R22E. The fluid ran approximately 400 feet down the main road then into a dry wash. It went north in this dry wash approximately 1/2 a mile to the NENE of section 17. It ran approximately 0.76 miles. Where the dry wash crossed the road, crew was able to recover around 40 BBL. Also in the process of flushing the wash with fresh water and recovering the flushed water. Also using a hydro vac truck to clean the top layer where possible. DEQ DE further reports - DE inspected the site on the afternoon of 4/5/16. Cleanup is underway. No surface water was impacted. Line is out of service while crew excavates the break.
