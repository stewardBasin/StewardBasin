Title: Treated Injection Water Release, Myton

Date: 12/25/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a treated injection water release of 150 barrels off the pipeline right of way due to pipe corrosion. Material flowed into a dry drainage. The leak has been isolated. SITLA and DOGM were notified by Newfield. Area will be flushed and samples collected.
