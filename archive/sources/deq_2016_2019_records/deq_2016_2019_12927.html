Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12927

Title: Uintah County - Motor Oil

Date: 03/01/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Anardarko Uinta Mid-Stream

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of approximately 500-gallons of motor oil, of which, 100 gallons reached soil. The release occurred due to overfill at the compressor station (Antelope Flat). About 400-gallons was contained within the secondary containment skid at the station. Initial response included placement of absorbent pads and pumping with a vacuum truck. The remaining impacted soil will be recovered in a few days. No waterways were impacted.
