Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12607

Title: Production water spill, Neola

Date: 05/14/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Linn Energy

Nearest City: NEOLA

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

On Saturday May 14, 2016 at approximately 9:30 am, Lease Operator arrived on the Linn Energy 1-3A2 Central Battery in Neola, Utah. The Lease Operator found that the saltwater disposal had over filled and spilled a large amount of production water onto the location. It was found that AC power had failed; this prevented the pump from transferring the production water to the injection well. The backup DC power had also failed; this prevented the high fluid level alarm from being sent. Based on flowrates an estimation of +/- 2100 Bbl of production water was released. OtherDetail The production water left the 1-3A2 location on the south east side where lease road connects to the location. The fluid then followed the lease road to the next location, the Linn Energy 4-11A2 SWD. The water built up on location then left the south side of location. The fluid then traveled 490 feet off of location before stopping. I was unable to see any evidence of pooling. Dennis Ingram with the Utah Division of Oil, Gas and Mining was notified by telephone and notified of the release. Location on Map only proximate to Neola.
