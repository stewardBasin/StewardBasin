Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13250

Title: Tri-County - Ethylene Glycol release from pipeline

Date: 12/15/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.38097906903, -110.214079059921

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a leak was discovered in the trace line running alongside the company oil flow line. The leak resulted in the release of 750 gallons of ethylene glycol to the surrounding soil. Caller did not have information on how the leak occurred or how long the trace line had been leaking. A vac-truck is en-route to collect pooled product at the ground surface. Impacted soils will be excavated and disposed. Coordinate s for release location: 40.380982 North / 110.214079 West.
