Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13042

Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 07/05/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 227 barrels of treated injection water near the 7-2-9-15 well. The water left the right-of-way and traveled approximately 1,000 feet within a dry drainage. The line has been isolated for repair. The drainage will be flushed with clean water and a sample will be collected for analysis. The DEQ District Engineer, DOGM, and SITLA have been notified.
