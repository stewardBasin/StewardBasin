Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13808

Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil at Simplot

Date: 03/20/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Mine

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 30 gallons hydraulic oil from a ruptured hydraulic line on a piece of machinery. Hydraulic oil released to the soils. Contaminated soils will be excavated. No surface waters were impacted. Contact for Simplot is John Spencer (Simplot - Environmental Engineer) (435) 621-2629.
