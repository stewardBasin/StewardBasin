Incident Summary

Caller reported a 228 barrel treated injection water spill at Well #5-14-9-16, API# 4301332336. The spill impacted soil and left the right-of-way and flowed about 3900 feet down a dry sandy drainage area. Caller reported this incident to Amy Doebele (DOGM), Nathan Hall (DEQ), and Randy Bywater (BLM).
