Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13014

Title: Duchesne County - Produced water spill

Date: 06/05/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: ALTAMONT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a leak in a discharge line resulting in approximately 12 barrels of produced water that spilled onto the oil well pad and soil. They recovered 4 barrels and took this to IWM Disposal. The remaining 8 barrels went into the ground. GPS coordinates for the spill site are: 40.31776 N, -110.3962 W. Caller notified Kevin Okleberry, DWQ, Nathan Hall, DEQ DE, and Dennis Ingram, DOGM.
