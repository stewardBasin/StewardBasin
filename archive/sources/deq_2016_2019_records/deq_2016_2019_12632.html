Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12632

Title: Uintah Co - Waxy Crude

Date: 06/03/2016

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: LAPOINT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Well: Bolton 2-29A1E Gasket failed on fire tube of heater treater unit. Unit is inside bermed area. Estimated 20 barrels of crude oil was released. Crude was misting from point of failure. This is a waxy crude that sets up when it cools. Wind carried mist into nearby on-site irrigation canal. Canal flows off-site onto adjacent farmer's property. Farmer uses canal for irrigation. EP Energy (EPE) is coordinating with farmer to mitigate. EPE will (1) remove all visible material from the irrigation canal; (2) scrape all material from impacted soils around unit, treat it and return to production tanks.
