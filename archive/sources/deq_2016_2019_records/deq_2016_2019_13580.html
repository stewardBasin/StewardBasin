Title: Duchesne County Gycol Leak

Date: 08/19/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported a leak of approximately 83 gallons of glycol to the soil at a remote wellhead. No water bodies were threatened or impacted by the leak. At the time of the call the leak was contained and isolated. The wellhead is UTE FEE 2-20C5, API 43-013-30550. The latitude and longitude coordinates provided by the caller were incorrect. The UTM coordinates listed above were obtained by looking up the wellhead on the UDOGM website.
