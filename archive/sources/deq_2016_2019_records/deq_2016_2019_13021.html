Title: Uintah County Produced Water Release

Date: 06/13/2017

County: UINTAH

Company: QEP

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates: 40.08124311867, -109.570161577105

Incident Summary

The caller reported a release from a tank of 92 barrels of a produced water and condensate mix. The mix was approximately 90 barrels of produced water and 2 barrels of condensate. The entire release was contained in an earthen containment berm around the tank. No liquid was released from the containment berm and nothing left the site. There are no threats to water bodies or drains. The liquid soaked into the soil of the berm and the company is in the process of removing wet soils. The API No is: 43-047-33746.
