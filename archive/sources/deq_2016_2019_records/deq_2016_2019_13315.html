Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13315

Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 02/14/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.075969661176, -110.000243691736

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 20 barrel release of treated injection water from an underground line near Well# 5-34-8-17. The release let the right-of-way and traveled about 900 feet into a dry drainage. The line has been isolated and shut in. The release is located in a sensitive habitat (endangered cactus) - cleanup is on hold until the BLM completes a survey. BLM (Randy Bywater, Jessi Brunson - Botanist), DOGM (Lisha Cordova), and DEQ DE (Nathan Hall) have been contacted.
