Title: UPDATE altamont -- Diesel Fuel spill

Incident Summary

A truck transporting equipment hit a rock which punctured the fuel tank and caused the release of approximately 50 gallons of diesel fuel into the soil. No waterways have been impacted. Report has been updated with exact location.
