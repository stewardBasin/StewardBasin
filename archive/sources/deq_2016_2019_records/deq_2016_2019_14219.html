Report #: 14219

Title: Production Water Release - Duchesne

Date: 11/24/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of 17 barrels of salt water production water from a faulty valve. Impacts are to soil and a dry drainage bed in Rabbit Gulch. All water has soaked in and they are waiting for soil testing results to determine response actions. UPDATE: Company reported a revised estimate of 22 barrels released.
