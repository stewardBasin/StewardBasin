Title: Duchesne Cnty - Production water leak

Date: 04/16/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.35346662373, -110.195922204499

Incident Summary

Caller reported a pipeline leak resulting in a 15 barrel spill of production water on tribal land which may have impacted a swampy area downgradient from the spill. LT Environmental will collect soil samples for lab analysis and the pipeline will be repaired. Caller notified Kevin Okleberry, DWQ, Nathan Hall, DEQ DE, and Dennis Ingram, DOGM about this incident.
