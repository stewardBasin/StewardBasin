Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14004

Title: Myton -- Discharge of treated injection water

Date: 07/25/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Encana Oil

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

On July 25, 2019 at 4 PM, an underground steel pipeline leaked 95 barrels of tretated injection water, which ran off of the public right of way, and into a dry drainage ditch. The spill ran approximately 1,672 feet to the end of the drainage ditch.
