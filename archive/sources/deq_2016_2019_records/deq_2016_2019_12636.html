Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12636

Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water Line

Date: 06/06/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a treated injection water line leak resulting in a preliminary estimate of 35 barrels of treated water (about 50% river water and 50% treated injection water) that leaked off of the pad traveling northeast into a dry drainage. Then traveled approximately 3,000 feet down the drainage. Spill occurred near injection water well #3-36-8-16. Clean-up is underway. Then it will be flushed with fresh water and samples will be collected. The spill occurred on SITLA land and they have been notified. Caller also contacted Amy Doebele (DOGM).
