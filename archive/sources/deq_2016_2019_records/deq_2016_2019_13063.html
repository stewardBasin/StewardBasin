Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13063

Title: Tri-County - Treated injection water release

Date: 07/15/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that 300 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water from a pipeline release. Release ran off-site and entered a dry drainage. The release continued down the drainage and was mostly contained to an old dry stock water pond. An unknown quantity of the treated injection water did release from the pond and continued down the drainage. No surface waters were impacted. Clean-up crews on-scene were able to recover 260 bbls of treated injection water from the old stock water pond. Caller notified BLM, DOGM, and the UDEQ - District Engineer.
