Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Title: Duchesne Treated Injection Water Release

Date: 10/13/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Products Company

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that 450 barrels of treated injection water leaked from an under ground line at well site 14-25-8-16 (GPS coordinates North 40 degrees, 5', 0", West -110 degrees, 4', 17"). The water came to the surface of the ground, ran down an culvert and entered a dry drainage. The total path of the leak is 10,175 feet. No water bodies are threatened.
