Report #: 13734

Title: Roosevelt - Diesel Release

Date: 01/15/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Pilot Thomas Logistics

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a diesel release of an unknown amount. Diesel fuel was noticed in a transition sump that contained above-ground piping and below-ground piping. Product spilled from the sump to soils next to the sump. No waterways were impacted. All product lines have been turned off and they are trying to determine where the leak is coming from. Contaminated soil will be excavated and the sump will eventually be retested for tightness. This is an above-ground storage tank site (Facility ID# 9000055).
