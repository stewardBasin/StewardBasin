Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13135

Title: Uintah County - Hydraulic Fluid

Date: 09/02/2017

County: UINTAH

Company: Simplot Phosphates LLC

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.592125730011, -109.471130658786

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 25-gallon release of hydraulic fluid from a ruptured line on equipment in the mine area (near high wall and spoil wall). Hydraulic pressure was lost and the equipment operator stopped work to identify the problem. Upon further inspection, it was determined that the pressure loss was due to the ruptured line. The hydraulic fluid was combined with soil and placed in a containment bin for off-site disposal. No waterways were impacted. No other notifications except internal company notifications.
