Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13739

Title: Duchesne - Release of Ethylene glycol

Date: 01/19/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Berry Petroleum

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 50 gallons ethylene glycol at well location Lake Canyon Nelson Tribal-16-28-D-56. A loose hammer union fitting on the heat trace system was identified as the cause of the release. The heat trace system was newly installed and pressure tested 10-days before the release occurred. The system was filled with ethylene glycol on 1/18/19 and the release was discovered by workers the following day. Ethylene glycol released to the surrounding soils and was contained on the well pad. Impacted soils were excavated and disposed of at Environmental Energy Innovations, LLC.
