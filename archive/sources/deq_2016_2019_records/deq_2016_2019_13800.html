Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13800

Title: Duchesne -- Produced water spill

Date: 03/14/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Encana Corporation

Nearest City: IOKA

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A frozen produced water line ruptured, releasing an estimated 10 barrels of produced water into the berm. Some of the material leaked through a hole in the berm and mixed with runoff water on the pad. Spill has been contained and cleanup is in progress. Samples of the runoff water were collected to determine if any produced water left the pad.
