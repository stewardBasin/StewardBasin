Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13971

Title: UPALCO -- Produced water line leak

Date: 06/22/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: UPALCO

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A leak from a valve in a produced water disposal line resulted in the discharge of an estimated 23 barrels of produced water. The water covered the road and flowed into a nearby dry ditch.
