Incident Summary

Eric Miller from JR Simplot Company discovered a hydraulic fuel spill at 11:32 AM at Well B of the Mine Operations Pit located at 9401 N Hwy 191. He began spill containment and then reported the spill at 11:52 AM to the spill line. Duty officer then called and alerted DE Nathan Hall and notified DWM.
