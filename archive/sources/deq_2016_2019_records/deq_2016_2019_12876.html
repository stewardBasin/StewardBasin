Title: Duchesne County - Injection Water

Date: 01/19/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.059439361292, -110.049730376958

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 35 bbls from an injection water line near location 22-6-9-17 with API number 430-133-091-9. The release consisted of treated injection water. The release left the right-of-way and traveled approximately 1,000 feet down a dry drainage. The release was isolated and then repaired. The drainage will be flushed with about 130 bbls of fresh water.
