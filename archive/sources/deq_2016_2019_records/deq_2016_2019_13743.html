Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13743

Title: Duchesne -- Produced water spill

Date: 01/26/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A leak in the salt water disposal line caused the discharge of an estimated 10 barrels of produced water. The liquid flowed approximately 60 feet to the east of the discharge site. No waterways were affected.
