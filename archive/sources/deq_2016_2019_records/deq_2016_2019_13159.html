Title: Duchesne County -- Produced Water Release

Date: 08/15/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E& P Company, LP

Nearest City: BONETA

Coordinates: 40.327568943337, -110.346644766024

Incident Summary

A salt water disposal line on Well Allen 3-9B4 (API #43-013-50123) broke and released approximately 6 barrels of produced water onto the ground. The water pooled on a nearby dirt road. Due to an internal miscommunication the company did not report the discharge to UDEQ until today. DOGM Incident Report #4024.
