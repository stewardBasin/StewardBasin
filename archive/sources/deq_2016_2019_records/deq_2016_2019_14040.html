Title: Hydraulic Fluid - Vernal

Date: 08/12/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of approximately 35 gallons of hydraulic fluid from a broken line on a D-11 bulldozer. The spill was discovered and a catch pool was placed under the truck to contain the release. About 10-gallons have been captured. Impacts are to soils where they are tearing out old roads and hard surfaces at the mine site for reclamation. No waterways impacted.
