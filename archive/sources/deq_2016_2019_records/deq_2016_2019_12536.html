Title: Duchesne County Treated Injection Water Release

Date: 03/07/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported a release of 58 barrels of treated injection water. The water was mixed with 2/3 fresh water. The water ran 1581 feet down a dry drainage. The leaking line has been isolated and shut in. Crews are cleaning up the area of the release. The location of the release is well location 15-36-8-16. The GPS coordinates are: N 40 degrees, 4 minutes, 9.82 seconds by W 110 degrees, 3 minutes, 57.33 seconds.
