Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12546

Title: Myton - Treated Injection Water release

Date: 03/17/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.080010365394, -110.079023875129

Incident Summary

Caller reported that 20 barrels of treated injection water released from a pipeline near location 1A-35-8-16. The coordinates for the release were reported as 40 4' 48.06" N 110 4' 44.48" W. The treated injection water ran along an access road for approximately 75 feet before entering a dry drainage. Release was contained and the segment of pipeline was isolated. Amy Doebele (DOGM) met with Newfield Exploration on-scene. The BLM was additionally notified by Newfield Exploaration.
