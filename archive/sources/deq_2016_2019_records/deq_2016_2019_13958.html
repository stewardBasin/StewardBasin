Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13958

Title: Vernal - Diesel detected in GW above ISLs

Date: 07/02/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: unknown

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the discovery of diesel above initial screening levels (ISLs), at 1.54 mg/L, in groundwater during a subsurface investigation. She said the diesel may have come from historic gas stations upgradient to the site at 89 W. Main Street and 110 W. Main Street.
