Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12875

Title: Duchesne County - Release of drilling fluid

Date: 01/17/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: MOUNTAIN HOME

Coordinates: 40.368796828109, -110.368072589516

Incident Summary

Caller reported that the build up of pressure of drilling fluid in the return line resulted in the material blowing through the berm of the reserve pit. Drilling fluid released off-site and entered into an irrigation ditch. A total of 25 barrels of drilling fluid was released. Drilling fluid is a combination of fresh water and drilling gel but would not produce a sheen on water. Snowfall on the ground made evaluating or attempting to remediate release difficult. EP Energy notified Dennis Ingram (DOGM) and UDEQ DE Nathan Hall as well as the landowner for the irrigation ditch of the release. Dennis Ingram (DOGM) visited the site of the release and requested that samples be collected from impacted area to determine the level of remediation needed.
