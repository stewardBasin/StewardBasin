Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13247

Title: Uintah - Dumping Limestone

Date: 12/12/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Unknown

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller States that trucks are hauling processed limestone and spilled powdered limestone all over the road near the intersection of HWY 191 and Diamond Mountain Road. Caller states that in some places it is two inches thick and 100 yards long and they are concerned that the powder will get or already had gotten into a waterway. The caller states that it has been there for a few weeks. NRC# 1199663
