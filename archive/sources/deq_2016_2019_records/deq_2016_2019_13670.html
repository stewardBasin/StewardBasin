Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13670

Title: Tri-County - Treated Injection Water release

Date: 11/05/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.090315690404, -110.045453549094

Incident Summary

Caller reported that an underground steel pipeline developed a leak resulting in the release of 24 barrels of treated injection water. The released treated injection water surfaced and ran into a dry drainage near the pipeline corridor. Pipeline leak has been repaired and remediation will be completed in accordance with BLM guidance within 7-10 days.
