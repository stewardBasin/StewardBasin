Title: Ouray - Condensate Release

Date: 04/03/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Kerr McGee Oil and Gas Onshore LP

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a gas gathering line linked to one well ruptured, discovered on 4/3/19. It is thought that sometime during the winter the pipeline froze and ruptured when it thawed sometime last week. The rupture released an estimated 5 barrels of condensate that went off-site. The well name is Cige 20. It did get into a dry wash (Cottonwood Wash) but contained there. Most of the contaminated soil has been removed and they are in the process of removing the rest. Soil samples will be collected afterward to make sure the contamination has been cleaned up. Tyson Todd with SITLA and Richard Powell with DOGM were on-site today.
