Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12629

Title: Roosevelt - Newfield Injection Water Release

Date: 06/01/2016

County: UINTAH

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates: 40.094674959474, -109.916292687887

Incident Summary

Caller reported a line leak resulted in the release of 474 barrels of a fresh water and treated injection water mixture. Released material was reportedly primarily fresh water. Release left the right-of-way into an adjacent dry drainage traveled an additional 5400 feet down the drainage. Crews responded and flushed the drainage with fresh water. Confirmation soil samples will be collected following clean-up activities. Release occurred at Well Location 2-29-8-18. Newfield notified Randy Bywater (BLM), Amy Doebele (DOGM), and Scott Hacking (UDEQ) regarding the release.
