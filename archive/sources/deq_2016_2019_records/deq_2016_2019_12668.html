Title: Newfield Exploration - Treated Injection Water

Date: 06/29/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 30 bbls of treated injection water from a pipeline. Internal corrosion in the pipeline was the cause of the release. Treated injection water released off the well site and entered a dry drainage 1,000 feet downgradient. The well was shut-in and release isolated. Dry drainage was flushed with freshwater. Soil samples were collected from the drainage to assess any impacts to the soils. Randy Bywater (BLM) and Amy Doebele (DOGM) were notified of the release. Release location was Well ID 5-14-9-16. Lat/Long 40 deg 03' 5.45" N/110 deg 09' 1.3" W.
