Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13298

Title: Bluebell -- Produced water release

Date: 01/26/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.342586849457, -110.217409433581

Incident Summary

Leak in 8-inch produced water line resulted in the release of about 400 barrels of produced water. The water traveled about 400 yards to a frozen stock pond. The contaminated water and soil will be removed.
