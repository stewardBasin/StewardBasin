Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of 30 barrels of treated injection water from a leak in an underground steel pipe. The location is Oil Well #10-31-8-18 with a given 40-deg, 4-min, 24.68-sec N; 109-deg, 56-min, 12.18-sec W. The water crossed a right of way and into a dry drainage. Cleanup is being conducted under BLM guidance with rakes and shovels. Randy Bywater & Lisa Boyd with BLM were contacted on 9/16. Nathan Hall with UDEQ was contacted on 9/16. RIta Riser with Fish and Wildlife was contacted on 9/16. A UDOGM incident report was sent on 9/16 with Lisha Cordova as the contact.
