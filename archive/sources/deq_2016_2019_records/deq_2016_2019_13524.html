Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13524

Title: Tri-County - Production water spill

Date: 07/22/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Arnie Powell Trucking

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.333239625683, -110.17397144473

Incident Summary

Caller reported a truck rollover which caused about 75 barrels of irrigation water to spill into an irrigation ditch east of Bluebell. Spill location on map is approximate. Caller said he collected samples for lab analysis. Nathan Hall, DEQ Engineer, was notified of the spill and investigated the spill site. He was advised to notify the NRC if needed.
