Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12712

Title: Myton Injection Water Spill

Date: 08/05/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Production

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A treated injection water line at well location 1-23-9-15 broke. 40 barrels spilled and ran down the road into a dry drainage. Water flowed about 1500 ft down drainage. Line has been isolated and construction crew has been called to repair break and cleanup spill.
