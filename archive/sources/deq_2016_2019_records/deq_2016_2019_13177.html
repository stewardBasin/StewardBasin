Title: Treated Injection Water - Myton

Date: 10/09/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a line break on treated injection water line released 46 barrels. The water ran into a dry drainage. The impacted area is 1545 feet. The break has been isolated and will be excavated and repaired. The impacted soils will be remediated and sampled. Sample results will be submitted to Tri County Health. UDOGM (email) and BLM (Randy Bywater) have already been notified.
