Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13226

Title: Uintah County -- Crude oil discharge

Date: 11/16/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: QEP Resources, Inc.

Nearest City: OURAY

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Operator at the GH 5-21 site discovered a failure in a flow line which resulted in the loss of 14 barrels of crude oil and produced water. Discharge was contained on site and did not leave the pad.
