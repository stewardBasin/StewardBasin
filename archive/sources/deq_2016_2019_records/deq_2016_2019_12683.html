Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 42 bbls of treated injection water from a pipeline. Treated injection water released off the well sitealong the side of a road and entered a dry drainage 2,000 feet down gradient. The well was shut-in and release isolated. Dry drainage will be flushed with freshwater. Randy Bywater (BLM) and Amy Doebele (DOGM) were notified of the release. Release location was Well ID 8-34-8-17. Lat/Long 40 deg N 04' 37" / 109 deg 59' 11" W.
