Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13888

Title: Duschene County Production Water Leak

Date: 05/09/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that a crew conducting a survey of a pipeline discovered a leak of production water. The leak occured southwest of well site Ute 4-19A2. Approximately 986 barrels leaked. No water bodies were impacted or threatened. The line has been isolated and a crew was arriving at the site to determine what happened and initiate cleanup.
