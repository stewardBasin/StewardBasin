Title: Duchesne -- Produced Water and Crude Oil Spill

Date: 07/15/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Unknown

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.229513214816, -110.502758842509

Incident Summary

Crescent Point employees discovered a discharge of an estimated 10 barrels of produced water and 5 gallons of crude oil on or near their well pad. Based on tire tracks it is believed the material was illegally discharged by a large truck belonging to an unknown person or company. Crescent Point has begun cleanup of the spilled material.
