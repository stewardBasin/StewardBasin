Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 09/01/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 50 bbl release of treated injection water near well 42-1J-9-16. The leak came from a pipeline on the right-of-way. It traveled down the road to a dry stockpond. Cleanup is in progress. No waterways were impacted. Newfield contacted DOGM, BLM, and the DEQ DE.
