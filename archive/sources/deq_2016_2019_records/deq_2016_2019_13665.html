Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13665

Title: Produced water spill near Bluebell

Date: 11/03/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

A failure of an 8-inch transmission line resulted in the release of 800 to 1,000 barrels of produced water. The water ran into a hay field. The water will be recovered, the land flushed and samples collected.
