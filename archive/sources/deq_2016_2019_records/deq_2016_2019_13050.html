Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 07/07/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.033051720351, -110.195009513475

Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 145 barrels of treated injection water that ran off the right-of-way into a dry drainage ditch. Clean-up is underway with the line currently isolated. Completion of the repair is planned for Monday, July 10, 2017. No waterways were impacted. Caller also contacted Nathan Hall with DEQ, Amy Doebele with DOGM, and Randy Bywater with the BLM.
