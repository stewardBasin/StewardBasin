Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12985

Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil spill

Date: 05/08/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a broken hose on a shovel causing an estimated 100-gallon hydraulic oil release onto soil in their open pit phosphate operations area. No waterways were impacted. The contaminated soil will be excavated, stored in a truck bed, and eventually taken to a landfill.
