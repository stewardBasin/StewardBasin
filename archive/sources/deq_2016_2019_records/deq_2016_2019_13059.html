Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13059

Title: Tri-County - Newfield treated injection water

Date: 07/14/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.075835320244, -110.005570081533

Incident Summary

Caller reported a pipeline leak resulted in the release of of 116 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water. The treated injection water surfaced in the pipeline right-of-way and ran down a dry-drainage. No surface waters were impacted. Clean-up crews are en-route to remediate contaminated soils and the pipeline will be repaired early next week. Caller notified DOGM, BLM, and the DEQ District Engineer of the release.
