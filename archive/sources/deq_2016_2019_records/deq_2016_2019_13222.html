Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13222

Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil spill

Date: 11/22/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported an estimated 51-gallon hydraulic oil spill after a hose on a water truck came undone. The driver was unaware that this happened so a long stream of oil and water spilled down Panel E Hall Road, which is a dirt road. Due to this, caller said the spill is not able to be cleaned up. No waterways were impacted.
