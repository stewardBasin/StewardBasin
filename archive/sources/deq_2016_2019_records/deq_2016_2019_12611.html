Title: Myton - Treated injection water spill

Date: 05/18/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.203361115136, -110.062598937439

Incident Summary

Caller reported a treated injection water line leak that resulted in a 90-barrel injection water spill that went to a dry drainage ditch. No waterways were impacted. Caller notified the BLM and DOGM. The line has been isolated and repairs are being made. They will flush the area with clean water and take soil samples for lab analysis. Unable to locate well # 41-17-9-17 on map but caller said it is near Myton.
