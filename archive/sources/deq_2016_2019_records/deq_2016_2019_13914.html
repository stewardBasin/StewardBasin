Title: Vernal - Hydraulic Oil

Date: 05/24/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a hydraulic line ruptured on a dozer releasing approximately 45-50 gallons of hydraulic oil. Only soil onsite was impacted, no storm drains or waterways. They have used a spill containment kit and have the contaminated soil bermed off. They plan to call their third-party contractor who will come to pick up the contaminated soil. Location on map is approximate.
