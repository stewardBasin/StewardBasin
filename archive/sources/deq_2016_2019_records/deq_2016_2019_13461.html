Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13461

Title: Uintah County -- Produced water release

Date: 06/19/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Summit Midstream Partners, LLC

Nearest City: BONANZA

Coordinates: 39.805137840118, -109.07789919152

Incident Summary

From the NRC Report: CALLER STATED THAT APPROXIMATELY 3 BARRELS OF PRODUCED WATER AND OIL WAS RELEASED AT AN ABANDONED NATURAL GAS PIPELINE DUE TO A HOLE IN THE PIPELINE. PIPELINE HAS BEEN OUT OF SERVICE FOR 20 YEARS. CAUSE OF IS HOLE UNKNOWN AT THIS TIME BUT THOUGHT TO BE DUE TO CORROSION. RELEASE WAS ONTO GROUND NEXT TO EVACUATION CREEK.
