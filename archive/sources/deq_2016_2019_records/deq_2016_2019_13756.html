Title: Tabiona Propane

Date: 02/08/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Unknown

Nearest City: TABIONA

Coordinates: 40.357768576914, -110.719558707726

Incident Summary

Someone was clearing snow for a neighbor and ruptured a 300 gal propane tank with a tractor. The tank was filled the day before. The area around the release was evacuated. All but one resident was allowed to return home after air monitoring.
