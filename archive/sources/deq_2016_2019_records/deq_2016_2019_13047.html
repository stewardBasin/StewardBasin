Incident Summary

The caller reported a diesel release greater than 25 gallons from an aboveground storage tank during offloading. Initial notification went to UDEQ project manager who is currently unavailable. So this notification was performed in order to complete cleanup activities. Initial cleanup activities were performed using absorbent material. No waterways were impacted. The fire department was notified and responded. The caller reported that the fire department approved the initial cleanup activities. A consultant is now involved to finish the remaining cleanup activities.
