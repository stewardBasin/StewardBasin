Title: Vernal - Oil Spill

Date: 02/04/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Rocky Mountain Power

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a small truck hit a power pole that was holding a transformer which fell down and released about 30 gallons of oil. No waterways or storm drains were impacted. Most of the oil was released onto asphalt with a small amount of soil being impacted. Pacific West will removing the impacted snow and will dig out the soil where the oil pooled.
