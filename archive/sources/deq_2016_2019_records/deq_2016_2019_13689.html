Incident Summary

The caller reported the packing blew out on a pump at satellite battery #1 located near SL T7S R23E S1. The material left the site and breached the berm. The release traveled approximately 0.5-mile before stopping. The oil setup and the water infiltrated the ground. BLM (Randy Bywater) has been contacted. DOGM will be contacted but not at this time. No waterways were impacted. Cleanup is underway.
