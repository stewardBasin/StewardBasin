Title: hydraulic oil spill near Vernal

Date: 09/17/2017

County: UINTAH

Incident Summary

Failed O ring in bulldozer caused the release of about 50 gallons of hydraulic oil to the soil. The contaminated soil will be excavated and placed on the appropriate pile for disposal.
