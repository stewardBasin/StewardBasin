Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14254

Title: UPDATE: Jensen -- Produced water/crude oil spill

Date: 12/17/2019

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Citation Oil and Gas

Nearest City: JENSEN

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Three-inch valve on production water tank failed causing release of 400 bbl of water and 40 bbl oil. 350 bbl water and 20 bbl oil were held by containment. 50 bbl water and 20 bbl oil flowed 0.6 miles to a dry wash (that was completely dry prior to the release). Some of the water fraction soaked in. The oil fraction, is thick and viscous, set up on top. Crews were able to recover some water and are working on recovering the oil.
