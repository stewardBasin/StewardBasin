Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13024

Title: Uintah County Produced Water Release

Date: 06/16/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: QEP

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that approximately 65 barrels of produced water was released from a buried line at injection site RW33-22B. The water left the site and entered a dry drainage. No water bodies or drains are threaten or impacted. The well has been shut in and the release stopped. The cause is unknown and the caller did not know when a crew would be on site to investigate the cause, remediate the release and make repairs. Possibly this weekend or first thing on Monday.
