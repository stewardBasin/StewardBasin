Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13400

Title: Vernal - Produced water and Crude Oil release

Date: 05/06/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: QEP Energy Co.

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.186558139633, -109.305090950301

Incident Summary

Caller reported an estimated 1600 barrel release of produced water which ran down a dry drainage ditch about 3.7 miles. The release occurred from a buried water injection line which has been isolated and shut down. No waterways were impacted. Crews are en route to investigate and assist in clean-up. Caller provided lat/long coordinates: Lat: 40.186234; Long: -109.306026. Location marked on map is not accurate. From Duplicate Incident number generated from the NCS report The caller reported the following to the NRC: AT 2330MDT ON 05MAY2018, AN ESTIMATED 1600 BARRELS OF A PRODUCED WATER AND CRUDE MIXTURE DISCHARGED FROM A BURIED INJECTION LINE (TO A WELL), DUE TO UNKNOWN CAUSES. THE SUSPECTED AMOUNT OF PRODUCT IS 100BBLS OF CRUDE AND 1500BBLS OF PRODUCED WATER, HOWEVER, THE NUMBERS HAVE NOT BEEN CONFIRMED. ALL OF THE PRODUCT HAS IMPACTED A DRY DRAINAGE DITCH AND TRAVELED APPROXIMATELY 3.7 MILES OFF-SITE. * INCIDENT WAS DISCOVERED ON 06MAY2018 AT 0830MDT. Remedial actions include the following: * TEMPORARY CONTAINMENT IN THE DRY DRAINAGE, IN THE PROCESS OF HYDRO-VACUUMING THE FLUID. * THEN WILL PROCEED WITH CLEAN-UP. * LINE HAS BEEN ISOLATED AND SHUT-IN.
