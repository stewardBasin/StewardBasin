Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil spill

Date: 03/11/2018

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported an estimated 40-50 gallon hydraulic oil spill onto soil when a hose on a bulldozer ruptured. No waterways were impacted. The contaminated dirt will be cleaned up when the bulldozer can be moved.
