Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13202

Title: 73 barrels injection water lost near Myton

Date: 10/30/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Production

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.084457407397, -110.014007012867

Incident Summary

About 73 barrels of treated injection water was lost from an underground transfer line to a dry wash. Cleanup should be completed tomorrow. DOGM, BLM and the DEQ district engineer were notified by the company.
