Title: Myton - Treated injection water spill

Date: 05/08/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.079165516657, -110.04807224325

Incident Summary

The caller reported an injection line leak causing a 43-barrel treated injection water spill at well location #2-31-8-17. The water ran 1100' down dry drainage and did not impact surface water. GPS coordinates are: 40 degrees north, 4 minutes, 45 seconds; 110 degrees west, 2 minutes, 53 seconds. Soil samples will be taken for lab analysis and the impacted area will be flushed with clean water. Caller notified Amy Doebele, DOGM, Randy Bywater, BLM, and Nathan Hall, DEQ DE.
