Title: H2S Gas Odor - Duchesne

Date: 03/21/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Blue Bench Disposal Extraction Well

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a recurring hydrogen sulfide gas odor.A at her home that is likely coming from a nearby blue bench disposal extraction well. She called a couple weeks ago (March 4th) and reported the same issue, but no incident found in the database. Nathan Hall came out on March 7th with Dennis Ingram of DOGM for a preliminary inspection and talked Bernise's son Greg, then visited the surrounding wells on March 14th with Brian Speer of DAQ to look for VOC plumes using their IR camera. The injection well was not in use at the time, but the thief hatches on tanks for both oil wells north and south of the house were leaking. He left a card with Greg. I called Nathan on his cell and left a message. Also, called Duchesne County after hours number and they said the would contact Mrs. Pilling tonight. Mrs. Pilling called back and indicated the wind has shifted directions and they are able to breathe again and the odors have subsided.
