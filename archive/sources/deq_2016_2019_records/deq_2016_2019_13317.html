Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13317

Title: Uintah County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 02/16/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.088483345282, -109.964307691925

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 75 bbl treated injection water release which surfaced on the right-of-way then traveled down the road about 1,000 feet into a drainage with frozen snow and ice. The caller stated that the release was contained by damming. The release also contained black iron oxide material. Water samples were collected. This area contains a critical species of cactus. Notifications were to the following people: Nathan Hall (DEQ DE by phone), Randy Bywater (BLM by phone), Jessi Brunson (BLM by e-mail), Rita Reisor (USFWS by e-mail), and Lisha Cordova (DOGM by e-mail).
