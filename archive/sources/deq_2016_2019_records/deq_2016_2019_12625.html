Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a release of 15 barrels of produced water and oil. The operator on site found a hole in the fire tube in the heater treater. The release was stopped and repaired. The Health and Safety team was contacted and cleanup has begun and should be complete Thursday. All impacts are limited to on-site soils. Michelle contacted BLM, DOGM and Tri-County already.
