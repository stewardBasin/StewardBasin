Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12489

Title: JNT production water

Date: 01/14/2016

County: UINTAH

Company: JN Trucking Inc

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.3374309893, -109.631117900671

Incident Summary

A semi tanker was headed west on Hwy 40 and rolled as it made a left turn onto twelve mile road. Hauling 130 Barrels of production water. Approximately 70 barrels were pumped off the truck. The rest entered the wash. No running water in the wash, mostly because of the cold temperatures.
