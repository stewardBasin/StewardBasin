Incident Summary

Yesterday at approximately 12:00 pm a tank leak was discovered at the NBU 922-31N location. A condensate/water tank developed a hole in the bottom due to corrosion which lead to the release of 295 bbls. 290 bbls were recovered inside of the secondary containment. There appeared to be a bent area in the northwest corner of the steel containment where approximately 5 bbls leaked out side of the secondary containment berm. 2 bbls of produced water seeped through the location and ran approximately 700 ft. down a dry drainage. All released product was recovered except the 2 bbls that left location and due to the terrain and steepness of the area it was not possible to recover the released produced water. Out of the 295 bbls release there were approximately 2 bbls of condensate that were contained inside of the secondary containment. The cleanup for this release started at 12:30 pm. West Rock was able to control and contain the produced water that was leaking out of the damaged portion of the containment. They were also able to stop the produced water from seeping through and leaving location. Savage was able to drain out everything inside of the secondary containment and haul it for disposal. All liquid and contaminated soil outside of the secondary containment is being collected and taken off for disposal. All tanks were closed and drained so no farther produced can be released.
