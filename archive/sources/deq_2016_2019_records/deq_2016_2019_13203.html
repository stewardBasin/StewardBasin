Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13203

Title: Myton - Release of treated injection water

Date: 11/02/2017

County: UINTAH

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.101485672, -109.951486718798

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a leak developed in an underground pipeline releasing 124 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water. The release surfaced in the pipeline right-of-way and entered into a dry drainage. Clean-up crews will begin remediation at the location today (11/2/2017) or tomorrow (11/3/2017) and make repairs to the pipeline. Release is located on BLM property near Well ID 10-24-8-17.
