Incident Summary

Caller reported a split in a line releasing approximately 45 barrels (7 oil, 38 water). The line was shut in and a vacuum truck was used to cleanup the initial release. Cleanup is ongoing at this time. Notiffications have been made to DOGM, BLM, and Tri-County Health Department.
