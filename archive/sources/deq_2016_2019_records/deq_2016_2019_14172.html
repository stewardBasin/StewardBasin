Title: Tabiona Car

Incident Summary

A car was West bound on SR 35 in icy conditions about 0900. The car left the road and ended up in an irrigation canal on it roof. The canal was flowing water. The car lost about 1 gallon of motor oil. Fire Dept paged at 1046. Booms were placed in the canal by Tabiona fire dept.
