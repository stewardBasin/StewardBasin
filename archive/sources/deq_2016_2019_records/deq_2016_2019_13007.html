Report #: 13007

Title: Myton - Crude Oil Release

Date: 05/30/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a heating coil in a storage tank developed a leak resulting in the release of 200 barrels of crude oil. A small amount of crude oil left the well site and entered into a small dry culvert. No waterways were impacted by the release. Crews are on-site to recover/remediate the release. Release location Well ID 15-13-4-3W or API 4301331514.
