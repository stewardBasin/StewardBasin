Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a spill of 25 bbls of treated injection water from a broken water line. The water ran down the right of way, across the road, back to right of way and into a dry drainage. The drainage ended at an old dry stock pond. No live water was impacted. The leak has been repaired and cleanup crews are cleaning up the spill.
