Title: Duchesne Co - Treated Injection Water

Date: 01/15/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.033256042732, -110.023523452373

Incident Summary

Reporting a release of 80 barrels of treated injection water. Seventy-five barrels flowed to a dry drainage. No live water was present or impacted. Five barrels were recovered. Crew isolated the leak and is making repairs. Will flush drainage with fresh water and collect soil samples for remedial analysis.
