Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12945

Title: Duchesne - Release of Production water

Date: 03/24/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.267805719874, -110.360274980186

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 300 barrels of produced water from a line leak. Release traveled 600 feet downgradient before settling in a low spot in the terrain. Line was isolated and excavation will begin to determine the cause f the release. A vac-truck was used to collect standing production water. Soil remediation efforts are on-going. No surface waters were impacted from the release. Caller notified DWQ (Kevin Okleberry), Tri-county DE (Nathan Hall), and DOGM at the time of the release.
