Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12982

Title: Duchesne County - Produced water spill

Date: 05/05/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 300 barrel produced water spill at Monsen 1-21A3 well. The liquid flowed across a farmer's field into a stock pond. A clean-up crew from Roosevelt has been dispatched to the spill site to take samples for laboratory analysis. The GPS coordinates of this spill are: 40.38413 N, 110.22072 W. Caller notified Dennis Ingram, DOGM, and Nathan Hall, DEQ DE.
