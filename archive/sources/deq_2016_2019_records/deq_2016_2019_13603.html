Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13603

Title: Duchesne -- Produced water spill

Date: 09/05/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E&P Company, LP

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.224943615009, -110.439275234655

Incident Summary

A seal in the pump in the salt water line inside the pump house failed, releasing an estimated 23 barrels of produced water. The discharge was confined by the containment berm and did not leave the pad or enter the environment. Vacuum trucks recovered an estimated 5 barrels.
