Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13656

Title: Injection water spill near Myton

Date: 10/24/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Production

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.072312197641, -110.034270907212

Incident Summary

A buried pipeline break caused the release of 18 barrels of treated injection water to the soil. The spill ran 2000 feet down a dry wash. The pipeline has been repaired Newfield is waiting for SITLA recommendations before remediating the release.
