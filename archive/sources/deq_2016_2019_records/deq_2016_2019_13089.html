Incident Summary

Report from DWR of possible Harmful Algal Bloom on Matt Warner Reservoir. District Engineer and Tri-County HD inspected lake on 5 Aug 17. Numerous dead fish and crayfish observed along the shore or floating in the water, along with characteristic blue stains on shoreline. The bloom appears to have peaked several days earlier and water does not appear to be affected at this time except on the north end of the reservoir. Several cattle were seen drinking from the lake with no ill effects during the inspection.
