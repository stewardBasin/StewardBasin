Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 03/14/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 2 barrels of treated, injection water from a pipeline located near the Fenceline Federal 4-19-8-17 well site. The release occurred on an out of service pipeline which left the right-of-way and entered a dry ditch. BLM has been notified of the release.
