Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 09/07/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield

Nearest City: MYTON

Incident Summary

The caller reported a 25 bbl release of treated injection water near well 2-25-8-16. The release left the right-of-way and traveled into a dry drainage. No waterways were impacted. The line has been isolated and cleanup is underway. The line will be repaired early next week.
