Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 52 barrels of treated injection water. Release flowed along ROW into a dry drainage. The line has been isolated and they will flush the area with fresh water and then take soil samples for lab analysis. Caller said that he contacted Randy Bywater with BLM and Amy G? with DOGM. Any indicated she would inspect the site and take photos sometime tomorrow.
