Title: 300 Barrels Produced Water Near Duchesne

Incident Summary

About 300 barrels of production water spilled at a production well. About 100 barrels flowed into a nearby canyon. The caller said a DEQ representative is on site although the caller could not identify that person. A cleanup plan is being formulated. The APR well unit number is 43-013-33972.
