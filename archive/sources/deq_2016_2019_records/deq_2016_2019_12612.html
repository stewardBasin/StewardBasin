Title: Myton - Treated water line leak

Date: 05/19/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.050438017727, -110.135917647773

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 20 barrel treated water spill from a line leak that ran off a right-of-way into a stock pond. Crews were dispatched. The line was isolated and the pond was vacuumed dry, then flushed with fresh water and vacuumed again. No livestock were impacted. Soil samples will be collected for lab analysis.
