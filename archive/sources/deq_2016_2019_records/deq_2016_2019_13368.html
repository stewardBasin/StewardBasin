Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13368

Title: Uintah County -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 04/04/2018

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Water contractor was pumping treated effluent from Roosevelt City to frac location for well injection. LayFlat line developed pin hole due to material fatigue, discharging an estimated 10 barrels onto the road right-of-way and soil. Hole was patched and line put back into service, contractor will replace line as soon as possible.
