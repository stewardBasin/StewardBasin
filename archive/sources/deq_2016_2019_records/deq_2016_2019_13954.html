Title: Duchesne -- Historic oil spill

Date: 06/17/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Unknown

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates: 40.189459019298, -110.39177849206

Incident Summary

Neighbor next to the property in question reported an oil-like substance "seephole" to Duchesne County that had been there for several years. DEQ DE went to the site and investigated and determined it is likely due to illegal disposal of crude oil or oil industry waste on the ground. Spill covers an area approximately 20 feet square, with a large pool of unknown oily mixed liquid about 8 feet by 15 feet. DE collected a sample for analysis.
