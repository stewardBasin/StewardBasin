Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13369

Title: Uinta County - Hydraulic Oil Spill

Date: 04/09/2018

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a spill of approximately 85-90 gallons of hydraulic fluid from a 1.5" hydraulic line on Hitachi EX18 shovel. Release occurred in one of the pits. Crew shut down the equipment. After shovel is repaired they will walk it off the area of impact to perform cleanup. No impact to surface waters. Facility has 100 ton haul truck to which they deposit excavating contaminated soils. When full that will be delivered to Uinta County Landfill for disposal.
