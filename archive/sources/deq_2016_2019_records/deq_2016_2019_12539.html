Incident Summary

NRC REPORTED: CALLER STATED A SALT WATER DISPOSAL LINE DEVELOPED A LEAK CAUSING THE RELEASE OF PRODUCED WATER TO THE GROUND AND TO A LOCAL STREAM. LAT 40 DEG 17 MIN 52 SEC N AND LON 110 DEG 19 MIN 37 SEC W LOCATION ACTUALLY APPEARS TO BE CLOSER TO DUCHESNE CITY BUT REPORT INDICATED CLOSER TO ROOSEVELT. RP REPORTED: We had a swd pipeline spill from the Brotherson 2-15B4, coordinates are 40.297717,-110.327033. Approximately 200 bbls of produced water entered a ravine flowed to a catch basin built into the ravine and continued approximately ¼ mile downstream. We received notice of the leak on 3/8/16 around 8 pm. We noted an oil sheen on pond water. Andy Jones is the point of contact with EP Energy. Called the spill into the NRC, report #1142371, left messages with UDEQ, UDOGM, and on your voice mail. NRC REPORT 1142442 appears to be a duplicate report. Address given as 17650 West 200 South, Duchesne, UT. Cordell Hamilton 435-531-8992 made report. UPDATE ON RELEASE QUANTITY - Ximena Simmons called to report the initial estimate of 200 bbls was low and the actual release was 1,000 barrels.
