Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12676

Title: Duschesne Co. - Produced water release

Date: 07/04/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy

Nearest City: BLUEBELL

Coordinates: 40.312428699844, -110.214085836811

Incident Summary

Caller reported that a line leak resulted in the release of 90 barrels (bbls) of production water. The release surfaced next to a road and entered a culvert crossing underneath 11850 West. The release discharged out the other side of the culvert and ran down a dry wash 1,600 feet. 50 bbls of the released production water was recovered by water trucks and taken to IVM for disposal. The line was taken out of service and booms deployed to prevent further migration of the production water. No sheens were observed on the released material. Near Sandwash Reservoir. Lat/Long coordinates for the Bodrero 2-15B3 well are as follows: 40.3124417 N / 100.2140916 W. Dennis Ingram (DOGM) was notified by EP Energy of the release and contacted Darrin Brown (Tri-County). on 7/5/2016 at 09:50. Darrin Brown initially filed the information under INR# 12674. The information from that report has been incorporated into this Incident Report.
