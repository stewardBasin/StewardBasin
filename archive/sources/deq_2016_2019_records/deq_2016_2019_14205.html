Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14205

Title: Mt. Emmons -- Produced water line leak

Date: 11/13/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: EP Energy E&P Company

Nearest City: MT EMMONS

Coordinates: 40.325644183648, -110.284747342213

Incident Summary

An anomaly in a 10-inch line was being investigated when an excavator struck the line and it started to leak. A water truck recovered approximately 10 barrels of produced water. Line was repaired and put back into service.
