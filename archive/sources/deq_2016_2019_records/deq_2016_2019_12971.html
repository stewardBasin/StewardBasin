Report #: 12971

Title: Uintah County Hydraulic Oil Leak

Date: 04/19/2017

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported that an unknown amount of hydraulic oil sprayed from a line on a piece of mining equipment onto soil. He estimates that the amount is probably less than 25 gallons but will not know until the equipment is repaired and the oil tank refilled. There was no impact to any water bodies or drains. They will handle the clean up.
