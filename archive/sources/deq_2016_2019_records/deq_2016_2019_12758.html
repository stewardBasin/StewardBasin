Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12758

Title: Second Injection water spill near Myton

Date: 09/21/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Energy

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Internal corrosion in a pipeline caused a release of 322 barrels of treated injection water. The release flowed 3,245 feet down a dry wash to a cattle pond and then overflowed the pond to a second pond. Both ponds will be drained and flushed and the soil will be sampled to ensure adequate cleanup.
