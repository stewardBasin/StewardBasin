Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 14259

Title: Uintah County Hydraulic Oil Spill

Date: 12/31/2019

County: UINTAH

Company: Simplot Phosphates

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.593620917251, -109.467716804687

Incident Summary

The caller reported that due to an equipment fire there was a release of hydraulic oil to the ground in an area of overburden at the mine site. The mine site is at the address recorded above. Approximately 10 to 25 gallons of oil were spilled. No water bodies or drains were impacted. The spill has been contained and will be cleaned up as soon as the damaged equipment can be moved.
