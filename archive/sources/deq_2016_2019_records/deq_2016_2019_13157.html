Incident Summary

Caller reported a 15 barrel release of treated injection water due to the corrosion of an underground pipeline, near well 11-7-9-18. The released left the right-of-way and traveled about 100 feet into a flat area. No waterways were impacted. Clean up of the release is underway and the line will be repaired.
