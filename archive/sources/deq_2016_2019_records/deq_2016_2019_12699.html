Title: Tri County - Production water release

Date: 07/26/2016

County: UINTAH

Company: QEP

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.193045067797, -109.283126251374

Incident Summary

Caller reported a 30 barrel spill consisting of 5 barrels of oil and 25 barrels of produced water. This occurred at the RW13-24B redwash field location. The spill was fully contained and did not leave the location. Caller said there was a crack in the well. The well has been watched and will be repaired. Only surface soils were impacted; no waterways. Clean-up is underway.
