Title: Duchesne County - Production Water

Date: 11/18/2018

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Altamont Energy

Nearest City: ROOSEVELT

Coordinates: 40.325649339932, -110.020970387335

Incident Summary

The caller reported a release of salt water from the salt disposal system. Cause is unknown but suspected pipe rupture. Total quantity unknown. The pipeline has been isolated and shut in while repair is completed. Initially, a water tank was brought in to flush the area. A reclamation company has been contacted and should arrive on 11/19/18. DOGM and DEQ have been contacted.
