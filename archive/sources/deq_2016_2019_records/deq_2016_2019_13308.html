Incident Summary

Caller reports a driver was fueling truck and it overflowed. Volume estimated 30-50 gallons. Flowed into trench near dispensers. Some also flowed into grassy area near a retention pond. None entered the pond. Environmental Restoration has been dispatched to do cleanup.
