Title: Myton -- Injection Water Spill

Date: 10/16/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Encana Oil and Gas (USA), Inc.

Incident Summary

An buried injection water pipeline leaked and released an estimated 77 gallons onto the right of way and into a dry drainage. BLM will assess potential impacts to any endangered cacti in area.
