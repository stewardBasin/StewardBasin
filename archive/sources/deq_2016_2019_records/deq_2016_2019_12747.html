Title: Treated Injection Water - Myton

Date: 09/12/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.034443128112, -110.091943947704

Incident Summary

Caller is reporting a line break on treated injection water line released 63 barrels. The water ran down the shoulder of the access road, crossing the road twice, and into a dry drainage. The impacted area is 1081 feet. The break has been isolated and will be excavated and repaired. The impacted soils will be remediated and sampled. Sample results will be submitted to Tri County Health. Amy Dobley with UDOGM and Randy Bywater with BLM have already been notified.
