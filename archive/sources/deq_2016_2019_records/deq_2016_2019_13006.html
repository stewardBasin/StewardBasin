Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13006

Title: Myton - Injection water release

Date: 05/29/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported a leak developed in an injection water line releasing 48 barrels (bbls) of treated injection water. Release left the right-of-way and ran 1,100 feet down a dry drainage. Crews isolated the leak and made repairs. Drainage was flushed with clean water and samples were collected from impacted soils. Release location is Well ID 1A-35-8-16 or API 4301331514.
