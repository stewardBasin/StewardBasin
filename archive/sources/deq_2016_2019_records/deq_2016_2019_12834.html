Title: Uintah Co. - QEP Produced Water release

Date: 11/28/2016

County: UINTAH

Company: QEP Energy

Nearest City: NAPLES

Coordinates: 40.191484720198, -109.442868786785

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 45 barrels (bbl) of production water at an injection station. Release was contained within the dirt berm secondary containment and did not release off-site. Vac-truck was used to recover standing water. Further remedial evaluation will occur today. Responsible party notified BLM and DOGM of the release.
