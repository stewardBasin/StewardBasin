Incident Summary

The caller reported a 166 barrel release of treated injection water from an underground pipeline (Well ID 3-24-8-17) which surfaced and ran into the dry drainage located in a sensitive cactus habitat. Cleanup is on hold until the BLM completes a survey of the area. The spill is contained. Notifications have been made with DOGM, BLM, Tri-County/DEQ DE, and USFWS.
