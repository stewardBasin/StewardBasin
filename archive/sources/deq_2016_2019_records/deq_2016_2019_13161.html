Title: Production Water Release, Myton

Date: 09/27/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Incident Summary

Underground injection line leaked 18 barrels of water. Some water ran off the right of way and down a dry drainage about 400 feet. Clean up crew has been dispatched. Line will be repaired asap and soil will be sampled after clean up. Mr. Thayne has reportedly notified BLM, DOGM, and the District Engineer.
