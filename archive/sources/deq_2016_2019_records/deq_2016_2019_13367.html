Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13367

Title: Uintah County -- Treated effluent discharge

Date: 04/03/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Crescent Point Energy

Nearest City: RANDLETT

Coordinates: 40.197329580493, -109.860287278991

Incident Summary

Water contractor was pumping treated effluent from Roosevelt City to frac location for well injection. LayFlat line was recently checked with a pig to clear the line but some ice built up in at coupling and the back pressure caused the line to split, releasing an estimated 15 barrels to the soils. Line split was repaired, ice was cleared, and line was put back into service. Contractor intends to replace line as soon as possible.
