Report #: 12606

Title: Production Water Leak, Altamont

Date: 05/12/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP Energy

Nearest City: TALMAGE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Production water pipeline leak. Approximately 339 bbls of produced water flowed from the buried pipeline and ran 1200 feet east on the ground surface. A trench was excavated at leak point, and 2 holes in the 10" pipeline were found. 2 clamps were installed to patch the holes temporarily and the pipeline was returned to service. (Photo documentation submitted by EP Energy) Landownership: Private Lead Agency: DWQ, Contact Ed Hickey
