Title: Duchesne County - Treated Injection Water

Date: 06/14/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates: 40.068874933306, -110.061103100557

Incident Summary

Caller reported release of treated injection water from line due to internal corrosion resulting in a total of 265 barrels at well site 16-36R-8-16. Approximately 110 barrels was released on location with 155 released off location toward the southeast. The release off location traveled about 3,513 feet in a dry drainage. The release will be remediated by flushing then confirmation samples will be collected. Approximately 110 gallons were recovered off location after the release. The release originated on SITLA land and terminated on BLM land. Jim Davis (SITLA), Amy Doebley (DOGM), and Randy Bywater (BLM) have been notified of the release.
