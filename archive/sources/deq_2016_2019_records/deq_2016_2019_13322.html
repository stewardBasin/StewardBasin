Report #: 13322

Title: Vernal - Hydraulic oil release

Date: 02/25/2018

County: UINTAH

Company: Simplot

Nearest City: VERNAL

Coordinates: 40.469569062348, -109.529011719453

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 25-30 gallons of hydraulic oil to the soils at the Vernal facility. Release occurred from a broken line on a piece of heavy equipment. Contaminated soils will be excavated and moved to the contaminated soil bank at the facility. No storm drains or waterways were impacted by the release.
