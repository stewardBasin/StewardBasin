Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13799

Title: Duchesne County - Crude Oil Spill

Date: 03/13/2019

County: DUCHESNE

Company: Altamont Energy Operating, LLC

Nearest City: MONARCH

Coordinates: 40.376878367499, -110.101310213149

Incident Summary

An oil tank leaked approximately 125 barrels of crude oil onto the ground. A hole in the containment berm allowed some of the oil to flow an estimated 100 feet down a road and into a cattle guard pit and ditch before solidifying.
