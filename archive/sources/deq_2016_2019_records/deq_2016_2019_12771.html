Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 583 bbls of treated injection water from the treated injection line system. Release ran down the shoulder of the road before entering a dry drainage and collecting in a settlement basin. Vac-trucks were used to collect the material pooled in the settlement basin and the area was flushed with fresh water. A soil sample will be collected to determine if further remediation is warranted. Release was caused by internal corrosion in the injection water line. Utah DOGM and BLM were additionally notified of the release by the RP. Release occurred at Well# 3-33-8-16. Location provided on the map is estimated from the Utah DOGM database.
