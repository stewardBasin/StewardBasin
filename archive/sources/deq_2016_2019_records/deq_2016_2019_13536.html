Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13536

Title: Oil spill in Napels

Date: 07/18/2018

County: UINTAH

Responsible party / company: Rhinehart Petroleum

Nearest City: NAPLES

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The Antea Group was performing soil sampling as part of due diligence activities and detected oil contamination in soil greater than the standard for total petroleum hydrocarbons for diesel range organics. Rhinehart Petroleum stores bulk amounts of petroleum.
