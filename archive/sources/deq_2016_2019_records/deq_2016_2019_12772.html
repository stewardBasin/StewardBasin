Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 12772

Title: Newfield - #2 Treated Injection Line Release

Date: 09/27/2016

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: Newfield Exploration

Nearest City: MYTON

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

Caller reported the release of 288 bbls of treated injection water from the treated injection line system. Release ran down the shoulder of the road before entering a dry drainage and collecting in a settlement basin. Vac-trucks were used to collect the material pooled in the settlement basin and the area was flushed with fresh water. A soil sample will be collected to determine if further remediation is warranted. Release was caused by internal corrosion in the injection water line. Utah DOGM and BLM were additionally notified of the release by the RP.
