Utah DEQ Environmental Incident Record

Report #: 13225

Title: Duchesne County - Ethylene Glycol

Date: 11/29/2017

County: DUCHESNE

Responsible party / company: EP ENERGY E&P COMPANY, L.P.

Nearest City: DUCHESNE

Coordinates:

Incident Summary

The caller reported via NRC the release of approximately 1,000 gallons of ethylene glycol from a pump. The release was to soil near the well pad. No release to waterway. UPDATE: According to Joe Araiza of EP Energy the entire amount was confined to the pad area.
