Incident Summary

Caller reported a release of 213 barrels of injection water from well location 3-20-9-16. The release left the right-of-way and traveled until it reached a dry drainage. The release did not encounter any surface water. Crews have isolated the line and repaired. The impacted areas will be flushed with clean water and sampled.
